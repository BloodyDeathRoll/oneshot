Use custom canvas/webGL to replace static image grids with interactive movement, fluid motion and canvas-rendered depth. Use editorial layouts, asymmetrical css grid designs, varying column grids and overlapping kinetic texts whenever it fits better than the repetitive hero and card framework. Generate a painless booking UX — hide complex booking engines behind sliding modal drawers or slide-outs, maintaining visual immersion until the user chooses to book.

Colour: cool, clean and saturated. Grounds are cool whites and light greys — never cream, beige, sand or ochre. One strong, pure colour carried large, taken from the place itself. One flat colour hit reserved for booking. No muted, dusty or earthy tones unless the business itself is that colour.

Type: clean geometric sans, light weights at large sizes, tight and precise spacing. No decorative or editorial serifs. No all-caps labels above headings.

Line and surface: thin, precise, black. Nothing soft — no decorative gradients, no drop shadows, no rounded cards.

Minimal, precise, extreme: one bold idea per page, and everything around it quiet.

Movement: the page answers the visitor — the pointer, the scroll, a tap. One orchestrated moment and small microinteractions, never a fade on every section.

For each website, create three mockups for a human to choose from. All three carry the same ambition and none is a template. The first is quiet: the idea carried by type, colour and composition, with almost no motion. The second is interactive, with movement and microinteractions. The third is full blown unique.
