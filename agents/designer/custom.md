Lead with the business's own video and best photography: full-bleed, clear, unobstructed. On the first screen, only the header, at most one short centred title, and one short call to action.

Colour: clean and bright, taken from the place. One strong booking colour, always with white text on it. Grounds are clean whites and light neutrals.

Type: a readable, tasteful text face for everything people read, plus one expressive display face (a handwritten script is welcome) for section titles only. Never more than two families. Consistent through the whole site, booking included.

Structure: distinct sections, each designed as a moment: rooms sold with galleries, levels with their footage, guests' words, the Instagram feed, a proper footer. Slideshows with dot navigation. Compact details. Buttons for actions, never underlined text.

Surface: no decorative lines, rules or borders; separate with space, colour and imagery. Soft is fine when it is tasteful: rounded corners on cards and buttons, gentle shadows where they help.

Motion: the video carries the movement. Small, useful microinteractions; no reveal animations on headings, no effects laid over the footage.

Booking lives inside the site and looks like it: choose the level, see what is included, see the rooms, pick dates, then hand off for guest details and payment.

For each website, make three mockups that all meet this bar and differ in personality: one quiet, one richer in interaction, one bolder. None is a template, and none is weaker than the business's current site.
