# Global Surf Lodge Kabalana — three mockups Shahar approved as THE bar (2026-09-30)

Shahar, 2026-09-30, on all three: *"This is how you design a website! I don't want to see anything less for any target!"*

The pages live at `sites/global-surf-adventures/mockups/{the-sign,try-your-week,postcard}/dist/` (home + `book/`). Contact sheets: `shots/global-surf/`. Take the **method and the level**, never the form (hard constraint 5).

## What makes them the bar
- **Hero:** the business's own footage, one direction, real speed, 6–20 s, sharp at every width; only the header, one short title centred horizontally and vertically, one short coral CTA ("Book package now"), white text on coral. Nothing else on the first screen.
- **Type:** a readable text face + an elegant script for section titles, tested on every title word; each mockup has its own pair.
- **Sections:** each designed as its own moment — full-bleed media moments, split layouts, level footage, rooms sold with 5–6 photo galleries, a composed guests section, Instagram, a real footer with buttons. No decorative lines, borders or underlines.
- **Booking inside the site:** level → rooms → dates & guests (Saturday chips, floating labels) → live total → hand-off to the engine with dates and guests pre-filled, JS off still works.
- **Three genuinely different designs:** own clip, palette, type pair, closing and footer each.
- **Numbers they met:** first load ≤ 0.9 MB before video, hero video ≤ 2 MB phone / ≤ 4 MB desktop after first paint, tap targets ≥ 44 px, zero engine calls on load.

## How they got there (to do it in fewer rounds next time)
- Shahar's written calibration (designer/references.md "Calibration …") was the brief; every designer re-checked it every round.
- The critics had to predict Shahar's verdict, not score rules; they only became useful once calibrated.
- Most rounds were spent on: hero clips (cut badly, reversed, too short, shared between variants), convergence between variants, and titles not centred. Get those right in round 1.
