A family runs this place, and you feel it about ten minutes after you drop your bag. Kabalana Beach is a five-minute walk — close enough to check the surf before breakfast and come back wet.

Dorms if you want the company, private rooms if you don't.

Dorms and private rooms

Mixed, female and male dormitories with bunk beds, plus double rooms and deluxe doubles. Every room has air conditioning and a fan, a private locker and a personal fridge. The bathrooms are modern, some of them open to the sky, and the showers are hot.

Breakfast changes every morning

Breakfast is included and it is Sri Lankan: rotis, hoppers, pancakes, eggs, a fruit platter, and a vegan option if you need one. It changes daily, which guests mention more often than anything else on the property.

What is here

A green garden and a chill zone. Free filtered water you can refill any time. Laundry. A minimarket on site. Scooter rental, with a surf rack if you ask. Luggage storage, lockers, and a 24-hour reception.

Pets are welcome on request at no charge. Children are not accommodated.

Getting here

Ahangama, a five-minute walk from Kabalana Beach. Koggala Airport is about 5 km away; Ahangama railway station is a little over 2 km. The property arranges a shuttle.
