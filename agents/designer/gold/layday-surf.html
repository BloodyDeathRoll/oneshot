Small, new, and about as close to the wave as you can sleep. One dorm, a garden, and a family who built the place and still run it.

It is quiet. Guests keep saying so, and they mean it as the main point.

One dorm, and that is deliberate

There is a single dormitory here. No second building, no crowd, nobody checking in at three in the morning. Everyone who writes about the place lands on the same two words: clean and quiet.

Every category on Hostelworld sits at 10 out of 10 — value, security, atmosphere, cleanliness, staff, location, facilities. Ten reviews is a small sample. It is also a perfect one.

The room

One air-conditioned dormitory, with real luggage space, a wardrobe, lockers and hot showers. There is a shared kitchen with a kettle, a dishwasher and everything you need to cook, plus a dining area, a terrace and a garden to sit in afterwards.
