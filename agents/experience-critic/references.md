## Calibration: Shahar's verdict on run 2026-09-globalsurf-02 (2026-09-29) — three mockups the critics passed at 8/10

The visual critics passed all three Global Surf mockups at 8/10; Shahar judged all three "still far off … the original is significantly better … no way we could sell this to a customer". Treat every point below as a hard bar. A page showing any of them is below 8.

**Hero**
- Title + one short CTA only. "See the weekly surf packages and dates" is far too long; "Book package now" is right. Any subtitle is very short ("All levels, steps from the beach").
- No rating in the hero. No tagline + rating + button stack over the video.
- Clear video with only the header on top, and at most a title centred horizontally and vertically — much smaller than a giant display word. A giant word over the video ("Kabalana") looks really bad.
- No decorative overlays on video (the WebGL "waterline"/reflection read as a mistake and added nothing).
- No title "breath"/reveal animations.

**Colour and type**
- Never black or blue text on orange. Use white on orange, or white on a darker orange.
- Fonts must be tasteful and readable; the original's mix — readable text face + handwritten script for section titles — is the bar. Barlow Condensed, Archivo-wide and Sora as used here were judged "not great".
- Keep fonts and colours consistent through the whole journey, including anything booking-related.

**Lines and links**
- No borders/rules under the header, no excessive lines, underlines and borders. Use buttons, not underlined text, for actions.
- Underlined footer links and a plain "WhatsApp" text link look outdated. Contact and WhatsApp must be designed properly (buttons/icons, a real footer).

**Sections**
- Distinct, well-designed sections, like the original; include the Instagram feed; a good footer.
- Slideshows with dot navigation, not hover-swapped images. Details compact, separated by " | ", not taking large space.
- Two-column blocks (text/numbers left, widget right) without stretched spacing or dividing lines (the forecast section).
- Text over video centred and vertically centred; CTAs aligned and designed, not floating.
- Rooms: gorgeous rooms must be sold — several images per room, description, enticing. Never one small photo.
- Package items/inclusions need no images (a list with icons at most).
- Forms: modern floating labels (Google-login style), not 1997 boxes.
- The booking journey must feel like part of the site, not detached.

**The bar:** the business's current site (tasteful fonts, handwritten titles, Instagram feed, distinct sections, good footer) was judged far better than all three mockups. A critic that passes a page Shahar rejects has failed its eval.
