# brief/mutag-meitiv/brief.md — the client's brief (station 1, inbound)

Client: מותג מיטיב (Mutag Meitiv, "brandforthebetter") — brand development for purpose-driven brands.
Current site: https://www.mutagmeitiv.com/ (Hebrew, `lang="he"`; built on Wix).
Relayed by Shahar in session, 2026-10-17. His words, verbatim:

> The current website is https://www.mutagmeitiv.com/ . You can grab all the needed information from
> there. It will be completely self hosted, CMS included. Nothing stays with Oneshot.
>
> Our goal is a complete redesign - a contemporary pleasant exciting design with motion and clear
> navigation and CTAs. Stay with the leading greens and the brand icon, but add mini animations and
> interaction. Keep things clean, tidy, airy and pleasant, with readable texts that is not too
> compressed. Informative but not overpowering with info, smartly divided and professionally designed.
