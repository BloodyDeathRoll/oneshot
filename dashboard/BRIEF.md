# BRIEF — OneShot dashboard

Written by Shahar, 2026-09-27. For this job it REPLACES `agents/designer/custom.md`, the
designer's house rules, and the creative director's "one thing" and sampled-palette rules. No
agent's `base.md` or `custom.md` is edited. This file is each agent's task layer. The run is
logged in `ledger.jsonl` as run `dashboard-01`. It is a PRODUCT UI, not a surf-business site.

## 1. WHO AND WHAT

One user: Shahar, the operator. He opens it to answer four questions, in this order:

  a. What needs me right now? (open gates: shortlist, mockup pick, send pitch, prompt diffs)
  b. Where is every business in the pipeline, and what is stuck?
  c. How good is the output? (critic scores, rounds needed, which agent keeps failing)
  d. Is it turning into money? (pitched, replies, meetings, clients, cost)

Every screen must answer its question within five seconds of looking.

## 2. DATA — REAL ONLY

Sources: `ledger.jsonl`, `run.yaml`, `sites/*/` (concepts, notes, shots, critic reports),
`agents/*/CHANGELOG.md`, `proposals/`.

- Write `dashboard/data.mjs`, which reads them and emits one `data.json`. List every field and
  its source in `dashboard/DATA.md`.
- Never invent a number. Where the ledger has no data yet (pitches, replies, cost), show a
  designed empty state that says what will appear and when.
- A mockup may use a fixture, but only one generated by `data.mjs` from the real ledger.

## 3. VIEWS

- **Overview:**
  - KPI cards: live sites, needs you, pitched, replies, clients, average critic score. Each
    shows a delta against the last run and a sparkline where history exists.
  - A funnel: researched → shortlisted → harvested → built → pitched → replied → client.
  - The "needs you" list, each item linking straight to its gate.
  - Recent activity.
- **Pipeline:** one row per business, with a progress bar across stations 1–9 and the gates, a
  state pill, the next step, the owner of that step (me or an agent), days in state, and a
  filter by run.
- **Sites:** a card per built site with its first screenshot, preview URL, critic score, a line
  chart of the score across rounds, and a link to the final-vs-picked sheets.
- **Agents:** per agent, prompt version, passes, fails, rounds needed per pass (bar chart),
  third-fail overrides, and the last CHANGELOG line. The copyeditor's repeated fails in
  `2026-09-ahangama-02` must be visible at a glance.
- **Decisions:** my gate decisions as a timeline, each with its reason.
- **Shared:** a left sidebar for the views, and a top bar with a run selector and a date range.
  Target 1440 and 1024 px; mobile is not required.

## 4. REFERENCES

- `dashboard/ref/*.png`: product dashboards. Open every one before anything else. These set the
  language: white cards on a cool light ground, one saturated accent with tints, big light
  numerals, small grey labels, pill tabs, status dots, sparklines, a bar chart with one
  highlighted bar, donuts and gauges for single percentages, tidy tables.
- The designer's own references and gold pages (`agents/designer/references.md`,
  `agents/designer/gold/*.html` and `*-notes.md`, and any reference images added under
  `agents/designer/`): Shahar's taste — cool whites, saturated pure blue, clean geometric sans,
  precise thin line, one bold move per screen. Use them for colour, type and motion quality.
  Do not use them for layout: a dashboard is not a landing page.

## 5. HARD RULES

- Charts are inline SVG built from `data.json`. No chart library, no chart images. Every chart
  has labels or axes, and a value on hover and on keyboard focus.
- One accent colour for all three variants: pure blue, around `#1F4BFF`, adjustable ±10%. Chart
  series are tints of it. Green and red only for deltas and status. No gradients as
  decoration.
- One type family, self-hosted, with tabular figures for every number.
- Cards: white, radius 12–20 px, no borders, at most one very soft shadow.
- No cream, no editorial serif, no all-caps labels over headings, no 01/02/03 numbering.
- Accessibility: AA contrast, full keyboard use, a visible focus ring, reduced motion honoured.
- Motion: numbers count up once on load, charts draw in once, hover states are instant. No
  looping animation.
- Speed: under 300 KB before data; `data.json` loads once.

## 6. THREE DIRECTIONS

The creative director proposes three directions. All three share the data, the views and §5,
and differ in how they look and feel. Each takes a different family from `dashboard/ref` as its
base:

  A. **"Instrument"** — dense and precise. Tables and small multiples do the work; think
     Sypher / Models / Nexus. Low radius, thin rules, many numbers visible at once.
  B. **"Soft cards"** — calm and roomy. Fewer, bigger cards and generous charts; think
     Shopeers / Logip / NexaFlow. The accent is used sparingly.
  C. **"Statement"** — expressive. One oversized number or chart per screen, bold accent
     blocks, pill navigation; think Nodus / FinGuard / Mondly. Still a working tool.

For each direction the creative director writes:

  - the idea in one sentence;
  - which references it draws from, and what exactly it takes from each;
  - palette hexes, typeface, radius and spacing scale;
  - the Overview layout as a grid sketch in text;
  - how each chart type is drawn;
  - what it leaves out on purpose.

Nothing from Shahar's surf-site gold pages may be copied as a layout.

## 7. DESIGNER — MOCKUPS

- Output: one self-contained HTML file per direction, at
  `dashboard/mockups/{instrument,soft-cards,statement}/dist/index.html`. Each mockup is the
  Overview in full plus the Pipeline view, driven by `data.json`.
- Look loop, per variant:
  1. Build.
  2. Run `node tools/shoot.mjs` at 1440 and 1024.
  3. Open the sheets beside 2–3 named references.
  4. Write in `notes.md` (look-log) what is concretely worse than the references: spacing,
     hierarchy, chart quality, alignment, type sizes.
  5. Revise.
  Do at least 3 rounds on the Overview before building Pipeline, and 1 more after.
  A round with no visible change fails.
- A flaw found in a look round is fixed or raised as a blocking conflict, never handed off.

## 8. CRITIC

Score each variant /100:

  - Data honesty (25): every number traced to `data.json` and `data.json` traced to the ledger;
    no invented values.
  - The four questions (25): time an observer to each answer on the Overview; name where the
    eye lands first.
  - Craft against references (25): specific, located gaps against named references, e.g.
    "KPI numerals 28px vs ~48px in Shopeers; card gap 12 vs 24". Evidence, not adjectives.
  - Charts (15): correct scales, labels, hover and focus values, empty states.
  - Accessibility and speed (10).

Hard fails: any invented number; a chart without labels; contrast under AA; a chart library.
Deliver the scores, the top 5 fixes per variant, and a contact sheet of the three Overviews
side by side.

## 9. GATE

Stop after the critic. Show Shahar the three Overviews side by side at 1440, each variant's
round-1 and final sheets, and the critic table. He picks one and gives a reason; only then are
the remaining views (Sites, Agents, Decisions) built in the picked direction, with the same look
loop and a final critic pass.
