# Direction B, "Gallery": notes

Built from `concepts.md` §3. One file: `index.html`, generated by `build.mjs` from `src.html` (layout and
script) plus the real data (`dashboard/data.json`), the real pitch drafts (`sites/<slug>/pitch/`) and Manrope
(base64). Rebuild: `node dashboard/data.mjs && node dashboard/overview-03/b/build.mjs`.
Thumbnails are referenced from `../../mockups/soft-cards/dist/img/` and are not copied.

## Assumptions

- **Draft parsing.** A ```text fence is the message and the rest is notes. KA Surf Hostel's WhatsApp file uses
  `---` lines instead of a fence ("the text between the lines"), so the same rule is applied to that. Files with
  neither (Global Surf's two, Layday's WhatsApp) are the message in full. The email's `**Subject:**` line is
  split out as its own copyable field.
- **Sender notes** ("Before you send") keep only the file's own plain-language advice. Sentences about test mode,
  gates, character counts, run ids and source files are dropped. The recipient number is pulled out as "To:".
- **In-person scripts** render as a readable document (headings, numbered points, lists, bold) with no markdown
  symbols. Two sentences in Lavish's intro (test mode, sources file) are dropped. Copy gives plain text.
- **Global Surf's email** contains the placeholder `[LINK TO THE PROPOSAL PAGE]`. It is shown highlighted, and
  "Before you send" says the pitch page is not online yet. That note is derived from the data, not invented.
- **"Waiting longest"**: KA Surf Hostel and Layday are tied at 10 days (same timestamp), so both get the outline
  and the pill. Marking only one would be a false claim.
- **Update status and Hand off** record in memory only (the mockup never POSTs). The page says so: "Preview copy ·
  changes last until you reload", and the popups' footnotes say so too. After Hand off, the request appears in
  the Handoffs rail with Copy prompt.
- **Sidebar links and "Approve or reject"** are not part of this file. They show a toast saying where they lead.

### Round 1

The shots are at 1440, 1024 and 390, plus the email popup (1440) and the WhatsApp popup (390).

- **Hierarchy is wrong.** The money band's `$0` is 64 px, weight 300, `#6B7280`, so at 1440 the eye goes first to
  the two purple-outlined thumbnails and the four purple buttons. Concepts §3 calls this risk out: "the money band
  must grow". Shopeers' numerals are about 2× their card label and read first. Ours reads third. → The numeral
  goes to 80 px and the band padding goes 30 → 34 px.
- **Too much purple.** On one screen there are the active tab, 2 outlines, 2 flags, 4 buttons, the rail's tinted
  box and the rail numeral. Mondly uses one purple card. → The rail box becomes neutral `#F5F7FB`, and the
  numeral alone carries the purple.
- **The sidebar's white column stops at 955 px** in the full-page shot. It is sticky at 100vh, so the white ends
  and the ground shows under it, which looks broken. → The body paints the column (a gradient at 240 / 72 px).
- **Bug: the email popup is cut off at 1440.** The box is taller than the viewport, so the footer ("Sent it?
  Update status") and the end of the message fall off-screen with no scroll. The cause is the grid parent: its
  `max-height:100%` did not resolve. → The parent becomes a flex container and the box body scrolls.
- **Bug: 390 scrolls sideways** (`scrollWidth` 439). The cause is the hidden tooltips, positioned off the right
  edge. → Tooltips use `display:none` until hover or focus. On phones they anchor left.
- **At 390 the header** wraps the summary to 3 lines beside the mode chip. → The chip goes under the title.
- **What holds up:** the 1024 rail-as-strip reads well. Card rows line up across all four cards (equal heights).
  The links, messages and record grammar is legible.

## Behaviour checked (Playwright, round 4 build)

- Enter on Suggested WhatsApp opens the popup and focuses ×.
- The copy button puts exactly the message on the clipboard, without the notes.
- Esc closes the popup and focus returns to the button that opened it. A veil click closes it, and so does ×.
- The inactive Pitch has `aria-disabled` and shows its tooltip on focus.
- Zero tabs do nothing on click and show their tooltip on hover. A tab sets `#ready` and survives a reload.
- No non-GET request is made, and there are no page errors. `scrollWidth` equals the viewport at 390, 1024 and
  1440.

## Known trade-offs (not blocking)

- At 1440 the first screen shows money, the tabs, the rail and the top of the first two cards, down to Preview
  website and Pitch. Messages and Update status for the first row start at about 975 px, just under the fold.
  This is inherent to B: concepts §3 lets the pictures carry the second look. A, as a list, gets actions above
  the fold.
- In the full-page shot at 1440, the rail column is empty beside the second row of cards. While scrolling it is
  sticky and stays in view, so this only shows in the full-page shot.
- Thumbnails depend on `dashboard/mockups/soft-cards/dist/img/*.jpg`, which is local and not in git. On a fresh
  clone they are blank until that dist is built.
