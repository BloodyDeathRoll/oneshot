# Price sheet — suggestion

The numbers live in `markets/lk/pricing.json` (Sri Lanka sites, USD): the offers, upsells, our costs,
terms and the open decisions. The dashboard shows them on its **Pricing** tab. One file per market
pack (proposal 2026-09-globalsurf-02/004).

Every number is Claude's first guess (2026-09-27), to be corrected by Shahar. Nothing here is in any
pitch yet. Once decided, the numbers go into the writer's `custom.md` via a proposal, and the pitch
drafts are regenerated. Inbound one-off clients get no price on the pitch (Shahar, 2026-10-17).

At 29 / month the marginal cost per client is close to zero (see `costs`): the price is for the
time, the pipeline and the promise to keep it alive. The setup fee covers the build tokens and the
rounds.
