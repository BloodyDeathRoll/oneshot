# lane: audience — who buys purpose-driven branding in Israel, and how they look for it

## Summary

Five buyer segments are visible: purpose-driven solo professionals (mostly women) who want personal branding; social businesses and impact ventures that need strategy and brand; amutot (nonprofits); public bodies and institutions; and organisations that book lectures and team workshops. Hebrew search behaviour cuts across what the client calls its two leading services. In autocomplete, "מיתוג אישי" (personal branding) mostly means personalised event merchandise, and "תכנון אסטרטגי" (strategic planning) mostly means municipal and government planning. So the site has to name both services with qualifiers ("מיתוג אישי לעסק", "בניית אסטרטגיה עסקית") and not with the bare terms. Willingness to pay is thin and is evidenced separately. Women solo business owners earn little, and accelerators and the state give some strategy help for free or at a subsidised rate.

Method note: search terms are Google autocomplete suggestions, fetched on 2026-10-17 from Google's public suggest endpoint (`suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=iw&gl=il&q=<term>`). They show which completions Google offers. They are **not** search volumes, and none are given or implied. "No completions" means Google offered nothing for that prefix. That is a weak sign of low query volume, not a measurement.

## Claims

### C-audience-01 — In Hebrew search, the bare term "מיתוג אישי" mostly means personalised event merchandise, not personal branding
- **Evidence:** For "מיתוג אישי" (personal branding / personalised branding) the completions are "ליום הולדת" (for a birthday), "על מוצרים" (on products), "לראש השנה" (for Rosh Hashana), "לאירועים" (for events), "לחלאקה", "לבר מצווה", "לחתונה", "למסיבה", "לבת מצווה". For "מיתוג אישי ב…" they are "בר מצווה", "באנגלית", "בלינקדאין", "בעיצוב אישי", "במבה מיתוג אישי", "בקבוק מיתוג אישי". Of 10 completions, only "בלינקדאין" (on LinkedIn) is about professional branding.
- **Source:** Google autocomplete, suggest endpoint (hl=iw, gl=il), queries "מיתוג אישי", "מיתוג אישי ל", "מיתוג אישי ב"
- **Date:** 2026-10
- **Confidence:** high (directly observed; reproducible)
- **Changes my mind:** Google Trends or Search Console data showing that the professional sense dominates query volume despite the completions; or completions that change materially in a later check.

### C-audience-02 — The professional sense of personal branding does have its own phrasings, and they are qualified
- **Evidence:** These prefixes each return themselves as a completion (Google recognises the phrase): "מיתוג אישי לעסק" (personal branding for a business), "בניית מותג אישי" (building a personal brand), "מותג אישי" (→ also "אישיות מותג", brand personality), "סדנת מיתוג אישי" (personal-branding workshop), "קורס מיתוג אישי" (personal-branding course, offered for "מיתוג אישי קורס"), "יועץ מיתוג אישי" (personal-branding consultant), "מיתוג אישי מקצועי" (professional personal branding), "מיתוג אישי בלינקדאין", and "מה זה מיתוג אישי" (what is personal branding). These return nothing: "מיתוג אישי למטפלים" (…for therapists), "…לעצמאים" (…for freelancers), "…לנשים" (…for women), "…ליועצים" (…for consultants), "…מחיר" (…price), "מיתוג למאמנים" (branding for coaches).
- **Source:** Google autocomplete, suggest endpoint (hl=iw, gl=il)
- **Date:** 2026-10
- **Confidence:** medium (presence is observed; an absence only suggests low volume)
- **Changes my mind:** Search-volume data showing that the audience-specific forms (therapists, women, freelancers) carry real volume despite having no completions.

### C-audience-03 — "Strategic planning" searches in Hebrew are dominated by the public sector and by jobs, and the business-owner phrasing is "בניית אסטרטגיה עסקית / שיווקית"
- **Evidence:** "תכנון אסטרטגי" (strategic planning) completes to "ברשויות המקומיות" (in local authorities), "עיריית תל אביב" (Tel Aviv municipality), "דרושים" (jobs), "משרד הבריאות" (Ministry of Health), "אגף תכנון אסטרטגי" (strategic planning division), and once to "תכנון אסטרטגיה עסקית" (business strategy planning). "תוכנית אסטרטגית" (strategic plan) completes to national and municipal plans ("לדיור 2040" (for housing, 2040), "משרד החינוך" (Ministry of Education), "הרצליה", "רכבת ישראל" (Israel Railways)). "ייעוץ אסטרטגי" (strategic consulting) and "יועץ אסטרטגי" (strategic consultant) complete to jobs, salaries, studies, McKinsey/Deloitte/KPMG and political consultants. The business-owner forms that do exist: "בניית אסטרטגיה שיווקית" (building a marketing strategy), "בניית אסטרטגיה עסקית" (building a business strategy), "בניית אסטרטגיה דיגיטלית" (building a digital strategy), "אסטרטגיה שיווקית לעסק" (marketing strategy for a business), "חברת מיתוג ואסטרטגיה" (branding and strategy firm). These return nothing: "תכנון אסטרטגי לעסק" (…for a business), "בניית תוכנית אסטרטגית" (building a strategic plan), "אסטרטגיה עסקית לעסק קטן" (business strategy for a small business).
- **Source:** Google autocomplete, suggest endpoint (hl=iw, gl=il)
- **Date:** 2026-10
- **Confidence:** high for the public-sector and jobs dominance; medium for which business phrasings matter
- **Changes my mind:** Search-volume data showing that business-owner intent dominates "תכנון אסטרטגי" despite the completions.

### C-audience-04 — Segment: purpose-driven solo professionals who want meaning in their work (the client's personal-branding buyer); trigger as the client frames it: wanting to do work with meaning, sharpened by recent crises
- **Evidence:** The client's own offer page names its audience: "א.נשים המחפשים לשלב משמעות ועשייה מיטיבה בעבודה" (people looking to combine meaning and beneficial action in their work). It frames recent crises as the moment to act, and opens with a free introductory call ("שיחת הכרות ללא תשלום"). The client's workshops page names "בעלי.ות עסקים שמרגישים קריאה חזקה לעבוד בעסק שמיטיב" (business owners who feel a strong calling to run a business that does good) and "אלו אשר רוצים לעשות שינוי בעולם דרך טיפול" (those who want to change the world through therapy/care, for a day retreat for care professionals). Testimonials on the offer page come from solo founders: a zero-waste events host, a sustainable-living brand, and "בונים לחיים".
- **Source:** Mutag Meitiv, https://www.mutagmeitiv.com/personal101 and https://www.mutagmeitiv.com/סדנאות-והרצאות
- **Date:** undated (live pages, read 2026-10-17)
- **Confidence:** medium (this is the client's statement of who it serves, not independent evidence of who buys)
- **Changes my mind:** The client's own records showing that most paying personal-branding clients are not solo purpose-driven founders (for example, mostly corporate employees or job seekers).

### C-audience-05 — The external trigger for that segment (career change toward meaningful work since October 2023) is reported, but on weak evidence
- **Evidence:** Maariv reports "גידול של 60% במספר הישראלים שהחליפו עבודה מאז אוקטובר 2023" (a 60% rise in the number of Israelis who changed jobs since October 2023). The figure comes from a 500-person survey run by a career adviser (Chayot Yogev), whose sample composition is not described. Respondents reportedly want a profession that fits their abilities "לצד תשוקה אמיתית לעשייה ולתחושת משמעות בעבודה" (alongside a real passion for the work and a sense of meaning).
- **Source:** Maariv, https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/israel/article-1341028
- **Date:** 2026-07
- **Confidence:** low (a single adviser-run survey of unclear method)
- **Changes my mind:** CBS or another representative survey showing no rise in career change or new self-employment since 2023.

### C-audience-06 — Willingness to pay (WTP), solo segment: most Israeli self-employed women run one-person businesses on low incomes, which caps what they can spend on branding
- **Evidence:** Adva Center, working from CBS data: in 2024 women were 37% of Israel's self-employed. 86% of self-employed women employ no one (65% of men). Among self-employed women without employees, 43.7% are in academic professions. The average monthly income of self-employed women in 2023 was ₪7,474 (men ₪14,153) and the median was ₪5,251. In the survey of women small-business owners, 60–70% reported an income drop in each crisis period studied, and only about half managed to adapt, partly for lack of "ליווי עסקי מתאים" (suitable business guidance).
- **Source:** Adva Center, "עצמאיות במשבר: נשים עצמאיות בעלות עסקים קטנים בישראל בתקופות של משבר כלכלי", https://adva.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/עצמאיות-במשבר.pdf
- **Date:** 2026-05
- **Confidence:** high for the figures; medium for applying them to the client's buyers (the survey sample is graduates of a women's economic-empowerment NGO's programmes, not branding clients)
- **Changes my mind:** Evidence that the client's personal-branding buyers are mainly higher-income professionals or companies paying for employees.

### C-audience-07 — Channel, solo segment: LinkedIn reaches about 2.95 million Israelis, mostly aged 25–34, and "מיתוג אישי בלינקדאין" is a recognised query
- **Evidence:** NapoleonCat, from LinkedIn's marketing API: 2,947,000 LinkedIn users in Israel (30.7% of the population) in September 2026. 25–34 is the largest group (47.5%), 35–54 is 27.8% and 55+ is 3.3%. Autocomplete offers "מיתוג אישי בלינקדאין" (personal branding on LinkedIn) as a completion (C-audience-01).
- **Source:** NapoleonCat, https://napoleoncat.com/stats/linkedin-users-in-israel/; Google autocomplete
- **Date:** 2026-09
- **Confidence:** medium (counts are ad-audience estimates, not active users; nothing here shows that the client's buyers come through LinkedIn)
- **Changes my mind:** The client's enquiry sources showing referrals or Facebook, not LinkedIn, bring personal-branding clients.

### C-audience-08 — Segment: social businesses and impact ventures; their documented gap is marketing and sales, and impact status is itself used as a marketing asset
- **Evidence:** The National Insurance Institute's evaluation of social businesses (2017–2021) gives three examples. Support for marginalised sellers included developing a website and help with "אריזתם ושיווקם באופן שיותאם לקהל היעד" (packaging and marketing them to fit the target audience). The value of registering as a benefit company is public recognition "שעשויה להיות מתורגמת לצורכי שיווק ומכירות" (which may be translated into marketing and sales). And in one case analysis, "המודל החברתי מושך לקוחות בעלי ראייה חברתית" (the social model attracts customers with a social outlook). Autocomplete shows this segment searches for the category itself: "עסק חברתי" (social business) → "דוגמאות" (examples), "בישראל" (in Israel), "מה זה" (what is it); "מיזם חברתי" (social venture) → "הקמת מיזם חברתי" (setting up a social venture); "יזמות חברתית" (social entrepreneurship) → "קורס יזמות חברתית" (course), "הכוורת יזמות חברתית" (The Hive); "חברה לתועלת הציבור" (public-benefit company) → "או עמותה" (or an amuta), "מיסוי" (taxation), "חוק" (law).
- **Source:** National Insurance Institute (ביטוח לאומי), "מחקר עסקים חברתיים וקרנות יוזמה, דוח מסכם למיזם 2017–2021" (Special Projects 183), https://www.btl.gov.il/Publications/Special_Activities_publications/Documents/mifal183.pdf; Google autocomplete
- **Date:** 2022-11 (report); 2026-10 (autocomplete)
- **Confidence:** medium (the report covers employment-focused social businesses, a narrower set than "purpose-driven brands")
- **Changes my mind:** Evidence that Israeli impact ventures name funding or operations, not marketing and positioning, as their main gap.

### C-audience-09 — Fear in the purpose-driven segment: being seen as cynical "impact branding" (greenwashing), against a backdrop of weak enforcement
- **Evidence:** The National Insurance Institute report summarises criticism of corporate social responsibility: "ספק בכנות כוונותיהם של העסקים, האשמתם בניצול ציני של האפיק למטרות מיתוג ושיווק" (doubt about the sincerity of businesses' intentions, accusing them of cynically using the channel for branding and marketing). Calcalist (2021) reports that Israel's only greenwashing guide (Ministry of Environmental Protection, 2014) went unenforced. It also reports that the Standards Institute's green label is long, complex and expensive, which "מונע מיצרנים קטנים לגשת אליו" (keeps small producers from applying). "גרינוושינג" (greenwashing) is a recognised autocomplete term.
- **Source:** National Insurance Institute report (as C-audience-08); Calcalist, https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/bjrk3tatu; Google autocomplete
- **Date:** 2022-11; 2021-07; 2026-10
- **Confidence:** low (this shows the criticism exists in the discourse; it does not show buyers of branding fear it)
- **Changes my mind:** Interviews or reviews from purpose-driven clients that never mention credibility or greenwashing as a worry.

### C-audience-10 — WTP, impact ventures: some strategy work is supplied free by accelerators and the state, which competes with paid strategic planning
- **Evidence:** Ynet on The Hive (הכוורת): accelerated entrepreneurs "מקבלים… תמיכה בבניית תוכנית עבודה תלת־שנתית למיזם, הגדרת אסטרטגיית הצמיחה" (receive support in building a three-year work plan for the venture and defining its growth strategy), alongside pilot funding and mentors. Autocomplete for small-business advice is price-led: "ייעוץ עסקי לעסקים קטנים" (business consulting for small businesses) → "בחינם" (free), "מחיר" (price), "מעוף" (MAOF, the state's subsidised small-business advice service); "בניית תוכנית עסקית" (building a business plan) → "חינם" (free), "ai", "מחיר" (price); "תוכנית עסקית" (business plan) → "להורדה בחינם" (free download), "לבנק" (for the bank).
- **Source:** Ynet, https://www.ynet.co.il/article/5671461; Google autocomplete
- **Date:** 2020-02 (Ynet, stale); 2026-10 (autocomplete)
- **Confidence:** medium for "free and subsidised alternatives exist and are searched for"; low for how much they reduce paid demand
- **Changes my mind:** Evidence that accelerator alumni hire paid brand-strategy studios at normal rates after the programme.

### C-audience-11 — Segment: amutot (nonprofits); they search for branding by sector name, and their funders judge them on public profiles
- **Evidence:** These are recognised completions: "מיתוג עמותה" (branding an amuta), "מיתוג לעמותות" (branding for amutot), "לוגו לעמותה" (logo for an amuta), "בניית אתר לעמותה" (building a website for an amuta), "גיוס כספים לעמותה" (fundraising for an amuta), and "איך מקימים עמותה" (how to set up an amuta). These return nothing: "מיתוג לעמותה" (the plan's suggested term), "אסטרטגיה לעמותה" (strategy for an amuta), "תכנית אסטרטגית לעמותה" (strategic plan for an amuta), "תוכנית עבודה לעמותה" (work plan for an amuta). On who decides: GuideStar Israel (Ministry of Justice with JDC Israel) states that its profiles serve "קרנות, תורמים, בעלי עניין ונציגי ועדות תמיכה במשרדי ממשלה ורשויות מקומיות… לצורך… קבלת החלטות לגבי מתן תרומות, תמיכות ומענקים" (foundations, donors, stakeholders and support committees in ministries and local authorities… for decisions on donations, support and grants).
- **Source:** Google autocomplete; GuideStar Israel, https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/guidestar_ministry_of_justice/he/Guidestar.pdf
- **Date:** 2026-10 (autocomplete); undated (GuideStar leaflet, treat as stale)
- **Confidence:** medium (search terms observed; that funders, not the amuta alone, shape the brand decision is inferred from GuideStar's own description)
- **Changes my mind:** Search data showing nonprofits look for strategy more than branding; evidence that funders do not look at a nonprofit's public presentation.

## Handoff notes

- **First screen (recommendation, not a claim):** speak to the purpose-driven owner or founder who wants a brand and a strategy for a business that does good. This is the meeting point of C-audience-04 (the client's own stated buyer) and C-audience-08 (impact ventures). It also matches the leading services in brief items 11–12. Lead with the service names in qualified form ("מיתוג אישי לעסק" / "בניית מותג אישי", "אסטרטגיה עסקית / תכנון אסטרטגי לעסק"), because the bare terms collide with event merchandise and municipal planning (C-audience-01, -03). Put a low-commitment first step on the first screen. The client already offers a free introductory call and a free intro workshop (C-audience-04, -13), which suits a price-sensitive solo audience (C-audience-06, -10). Give organisations (amutot, public bodies, lecture bookers) a clear second path, such as "לארגונים" (for organisations), rather than the first screen (C-audience-11, -12, -13).
- **For seo:** the terms worth targeting are in C-audience-02, -03, -08, -11 and -13. Avoid the bare "מיתוג אישי" as a page's sole H1 or title term (C-audience-01). Nonprofit pages should use "מיתוג לעמותות" / "מיתוג עמותה", not "מיתוג לעמותה" (C-audience-11). Lecture pages should say "הרצאות וסדנאות לארגונים" before the topic (C-audience-13).
- **For copy:** the credibility worry (C-audience-09) suggests proof over adjectives: named clients, real outcomes, sourced testimonials. That is speculation about conversion, to be tested.
- **Speculation:** the women-led share of the client's audience (its podcast on women, business and meaning; C-audience-06 figures) may matter for tone and imagery. Nothing here proves the client's buyers are mostly women. Ask the client.
- **Open questions for the client (for the merger):** (1) Which segment brings the most revenue today: individuals, impact ventures, amutot, public bodies, or lecture bookings? (2) Where do enquiries come from: LinkedIn, referrals, Facebook groups, accelerators? (3) Are the public-sector logos paid strategy or branding projects, or lectures?
