# lane: industry — purpose-driven / impact branding as a service category

## Summary

Small branding practices live mostly on fixed-price projects, with hourly consulting, courses and talks as secondary lines; retainers are a minority shape (C-industry-01). In Israel the observable price shapes are a prepaid bank of consulting hours, recorded courses around ₪1,000, talks and workshops priced on request, and a free government personal-branding course that sets a floor for basic know-how (C-industry-02 to C-industry-05). The forces now are a hard Israeli SMB and nonprofit climate (C-industry-06 to C-industry-08), purpose turning from "change the world" to "change my world" plus doubt about green claims (C-industry-09, C-industry-10), stricter green-claim rules abroad against weak ESG and green-claim pressure at home (C-industry-11 to C-industry-13), and AI that speeds design work but is not yet trusted (C-industry-14). LinkedIn reaches about 30 % of Israelis, so personal branding has a large channel (C-industry-15).

## Claims

### C-industry-01 — Small branding practices mostly charge fixed project prices; retainers are a minority shape, and consulting, digital products and courses are secondary revenue lines
- **Evidence:** State of Brand Report 2023, 267 branding professionals (55.8 % self-employed, 27.7 % founders). Pricing models: 69 % fixed price, 36.7 % hourly, 35.2 % value-based, 19.9 % retainers. Project prices: 50 % charge $500–$5k, 18.7 % under $500, 15.4 % $5k–$10k, 7.5 % above $20k. Other revenue streams: consulting 18.8 %, digital products 16.4 %, online courses about 7 %, none 16.2 %.
- **Source:** JustCreative, "State of Brand Report" — https://justcreative.com/?p=434367
- **Date:** 2023
- **Confidence:** medium (global, mostly English-speaking, self-selected sample; three years old)
- **Changes my mind:** an Israeli or post-2024 survey of branding freelancers showing retainers or courses as the main revenue line.

### C-industry-02 — In Israel, 1:1 branding guidance is sold as a prepaid bank of consulting hours, at about ₪750 per hour
- **Evidence:** A branding consultant's offer: "בנק של 10 שעות ייעוץ לליווי תהליך מיתוג" (a bank of 10 consulting hours to guide a branding process), meetings on Zoom ("הפגישות מתקיימות בזום"), on-site visits at extra cost, 7,500 ש"ח + VAT; "כמות השעות הסופית תקבע בהתאם להיקף הפרויקט" (the final number of hours depends on the project's scope).
- **Source:** Polina Stern-Shlomo, branding page — https://www.polinash.co.il/branding
- **Date:** undated (read 2026-10)
- **Confidence:** low (one practitioner; page undated)
- **Changes my mind:** several Israeli brand-strategy or personal-branding offers that publish prices well away from ₪500–1,000 per hour, or that sell fixed packages instead of hour banks.

### C-industry-03 — Recorded Hebrew branding courses list at about ₪1,000–1,250 and are often discounted; a long professional brand-manager course costs ₪13,500
- **Evidence:** Brand Academy: "סודות המיתוג המנצח בעידן החדש – 10 מפגשים לצפייה בזמן שלכם" (10 recorded sessions, watch any time), "מחיר רגיל 1050 ₪ (לא כולל מע"מ)" (list price ₪1,050 before VAT), with a "מחיר יולי לוהט רק 420 ₪" summer sale. The same shop lists ₪1,228 and ₪1,239 versions of digital courses, a "Strategic Branding Masterclass" at ₪1,111 and "שכר לימוד לקורס מנהלי מותג" (brand-manager course tuition) at ₪13,500.
- **Source:** Brand Academy course page — https://brandacademy.co.il/%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%93%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%92-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%A6%D7%97-%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%93%D7%9F-%D7%94%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9/
- **Date:** undated (read 2026-10; one product slug says "brand-friday-2024")
- **Confidence:** medium for the price shape; low as a market average (one provider)
- **Changes my mind:** a course marketplace showing Hebrew branding courses clustered far below or above ₪1,000.

### C-industry-04 — The Israeli state gives a free 3-hour personal-branding course, which sets a floor: paid personal branding has to sell more than basic know-how
- **Evidence:** Campus IL course "מיתוג אישי", offered by the Ministry of Labor with the Israel National Digital Agency and the Israeli Employment Service: "Free", "Self-Paced", "3 Hours", aimed at people who are "employees, self-employed, entrepreneurs or a combination of all".
- **Source:** Campus IL — https://campus.gov.il/en/course/molsa-gov-career-personalbranding101-he/
- **Date:** undated (live 2026-10)
- **Confidence:** high that it exists and is free; the "floor" reading is an inference
- **Changes my mind:** the course withdrawn, or evidence that buyers of paid personal branding never meet free material first.

### C-industry-05 — Talks and workshops for organizations are a standard shape in Israeli branding: a ~50-minute talk or ~90-minute workshop, price on request
- **Evidence:** A branding lecturer sells "הרצאה וסדנה – הקנקן ומה שיש בו – על מיתוג, שיווק, והאני האותנטי" (a talk and workshop on branding, marketing and the authentic self) as a talk of about 50 minutes or a workshop of about 90 minutes, for "עובדים, מנהלים, שכירים ועצמאים" (employees, managers, salaried and self-employed); no price shown. A second provider lists "סדנאות מותאמות אישית לארגונים" (custom workshops for organizations) and a brand-story workshop, also with no prices, only "צור קשר" (contact us).
- **Source:** Eyal Drori — https://eyaldrori.co.il/lecture-branding-marketing/ ; Rotem Kramin — https://www.swanets.com/%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%93%D7%A0%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA/
- **Date:** undated (read 2026-10)
- **Confidence:** medium (two providers, same shape)
- **Changes my mind:** Israeli lecture agencies showing that branding talks are usually sold with public prices or as public ticketed events, not to organizations.

### C-industry-06 — Israeli SMBs are in a record closure cycle: about 61,000 businesses closed in 2025 and 37,500 opened, mostly small and medium ones
- **Evidence:** "כ-61 אלף חברות ועסקים נסגרו במהלך השנה" (about 61,000 companies and businesses closed during the year); "נפתחו במהלך השנה כ-37.5 אלף עסקים חדשים בלבד" (only about 37,500 new businesses opened); "מרבית הסגירות נרשמו בקרב עסקים קטנים ובינוניים" (most closures were small and medium businesses). CofaceBDI CEO Roy Minkov quoted. For comparison, CofaceBDI put 2024 at about 59,000 closures against 40–42,000 in a normal year.
- **Source:** Mako, CofaceBDI 2025 summary — https://www.mako.co.il/news-money/2025_q4/Article-597ccc2d0cf4b91027.htm ; ice.co.il, "עסקים תחת מלחמה – סיכום שנה" — https://www.ice.co.il/bdispot/news/article/1045478
- **Date:** 2025-12 (2025 figures); 2025-01 (2024 figures)
- **Confidence:** high
- **Changes my mind:** 2026 CofaceBDI data showing closures back near the 40–42,000 norm.

### C-industry-07 — More than half of Israeli self-employed reported lower business activity since the war, down about 50 % on average
- **Evidence:** "יותר ממחצית מהעצמאים (54%) חווים ירידה בפעילות העסקית מאז המלחמה (ירידה ממוצעת של כ-50%)" (54 % of the self-employed have seen activity fall since the war, by about 50 % on average). Source line: IDI survey, December 2024, representative sample of 1,236 people employed before the war, 194 of them self-employed; deck dated 17.02.2025.
- **Source:** Israel Democracy Institute, "The situation of the self-employed in Israel" — https://www.idi.org.il/media/27471/the-situation-of-the-self-employed-in-israel.pdf
- **Date:** 2025-02 (survey 2024-12)
- **Confidence:** medium (small self-employed sub-sample; before the 2025 figures)
- **Changes my mind:** a 2026 IDI or CBS figure showing self-employed activity back to pre-war levels.

### C-industry-08 — Israeli nonprofits are under financial strain: 71 % report difficulty and 40 % report less money from abroad
- **Evidence:** Hebrew University's "סקר העמותות 2025" (Nonprofits Survey 2025), September–October 2025, 170 organizations: "71% מארגוני המגזר השלישי מדווחים על קשיים כלכליים" (71 % report financial difficulties); "40% דיווחו כי התמיכה מקרנות ותורמים מחו"ל ירדה" (40 % say support from foreign foundations and donors fell); 18 % cannot meet current monthly payments.
- **Source:** Calcalist — https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/hk40tzwgbx
- **Date:** 2025-11
- **Confidence:** medium (n = 170; reported in the press, not read from the survey itself)
- **Changes my mind:** the full survey showing the sample skewed to welfare organizations hit hardest, or 2026 data showing recovery.

### C-industry-09 — Purpose has moved from "change the world" to "change my world": consumers rank personal benefit above helping the community, and trust now matters as much as price and quality
- **Evidence:** Edelman Trust Barometer special report "Brand Trust, From We to Me" (2025), unveiled at Cannes Lions: trust "is equal to price and quality in brand purchase decisions"; 80 % trust the brands they use. Top expectations are "Make me feel good", "Provide hope for the future" and "Give me quality information" (68 % each), ahead of "Help me do good" (61 %) and "Help the community" (59 %). The report's sample, per Edelman's summary: 15,000 respondents, 15 countries, April–May 2025.
- **Source:** The Drum, 2025-06-19 — https://www.thedrum.com/news/2025/06/19/richard-edelman-brands-must-become-the-optimists-consumers-can-t-afford-be (Edelman's own pages returned 403 and could not be read)
- **Date:** 2025-06
- **Confidence:** medium (secondary report; Israel is not among the 15 countries named)
- **Changes my mind:** Edelman's 2026 edition reversing the shift, or Israeli data showing community and cause outranking personal benefit.

### C-industry-10 — Consumers doubt green messaging: 60 % believe companies use it purely for commercial reasons
- **Evidence:** Kantar's sustainability report (covered November 2025): "60 percent of consumers believe companies use green messaging for purely commercial purposes"; "83 percent of brands have a negative perception regarding sustainability".
- **Source:** FashionUnited — https://fashionunited.uk/news/business/cost-of-inaction-grows-for-your-brand-every-year-warns-kantar-in-its-latest-sustainability-report/2025110784773
- **Date:** 2025-11
- **Confidence:** medium (global; sample size not given in the article)
- **Changes my mind:** Kantar's primary report showing the 60 % figure comes from a narrow market set, or Israeli data showing the opposite.

### C-industry-11 — From 27 September 2026 the EU bans unproven generic green claims, and the ban covers non-EU traders selling to EU consumers
- **Evidence:** Directive (EU) 2024/825 (EmpCo): "Generic environmental claims, such as 'eco-friendly', 'green', or 'biobased', where a trader is not able to demonstrate recognised excellent environmental performance relevant to the claim are prohibited"; offset-based "neutral, reduced or positive impact" claims are banned; "Sustainability labels that are not based on a certification scheme or established by public authorities are prohibited". The rules apply "irrespective of where the entity making the claim is established". No transitional relief.
- **Source:** Latham & Watkins — https://www.lw.com/en/insights/eu-empowering-consumers-directive-new-rules-on-green-claims-apply-from-27-september-2026
- **Date:** 2026-09
- **Confidence:** high (on the rule; how many Israeli clients sell B2C into the EU is unknown)
- **Changes my mind:** a postponement or an amendment narrowing the scope.

### C-industry-12 — ESG reporting in Israel's capital market rests on a voluntary recommended outline, not an obligation
- **Evidence:** The Israel Securities Authority published its corporate-responsibility outline as a "recommended outline", and "the call to publish a corporate responsibility report is only on a voluntary basis"; it is "not … subject to the Securities Law, so as not to create new obligations". It points to SASB and GRI.
- **Source:** Barnea Jaffa Lande, client update — https://barlaw.co.il/practice_areas/regulation/corporate-governance-and-esg/client_updates/27579-2/
- **Date:** 2021-05
- **Confidence:** low (old; no newer ISA rule was found, but the search was not exhaustive)
- **Changes my mind:** a 2024–2026 ISA directive or law that makes ESG or climate disclosure mandatory for reporting corporations.

### C-industry-13 — B Corp is barely present in Israel: four certified Israeli companies and no local B Lab body, against about 9,600 B Corps worldwide
- **Evidence:** "Four" Israeli B Corps (UBQ Materials, Shamaym, 2B-Community, Impact First); B Lab "do not have an international department or a qualified Israeli entity to facilitate those connections". Worldwide: "9,576 Certified B Corporations across 160 industries in 102 countries" (March 2025).
- **Source:** Zavit, "Corporate sustainability: to B or not to B" — https://www.zavit.org.il/intl/en/economy_real_estate/corporate-sustainability-to-b-or-not-to-b/ ; Wikipedia, B Corporation (certification) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B_Corporation_(certification)
- **Date:** 2021-04 (Israel count); 2025-03 (world count)
- **Confidence:** low for the current Israeli count (5 years old; B Lab's directory returned 403); high that adoption is very small
- **Changes my mind:** B Lab's directory showing dozens of Israeli B Corps, or a B Lab Israel partner launched since 2021. The plan's premise "B Corp growth in Israel" is not supported by anything found.

### C-industry-14 — AI speeds design work but is not trusted in core design: 78 % say it makes them more efficient, 31 % of designers use it for core work like asset generation, and 32 % can depend on its output
- **Evidence:** Figma 2025 AI report, 2,500 Figma users: "78% … agree that 'AI significantly enhances the efficiency of my work'"; designers 69 % satisfied, 54 % see better quality (developers 82 % and 68 %); "only 31% of designers use AI in core design work like asset generation"; only 32 % can depend on AI outputs.
- **Source:** Figma blog — https://www.figma.com/blog/figma-2025-ai-report-perspectives/
- **Date:** 2025-04
- **Confidence:** medium (product designers, not brand strategists; a tool vendor's survey)
- **Changes my mind:** a 2026 survey of brand studios showing AI used for most core identity work, or Israeli clients choosing AI logo tools over studios.

### C-industry-15 — LinkedIn reaches about 2.9 million Israelis (30.7 % of the population), with 25–34 the largest group
- **Evidence:** "2 947 000 Linkedin users in Israel in September 2026", "30.7% of its entire population"; "People aged 25 to 34 were the largest user group (1 400 000)".
- **Source:** NapoleonCat — https://napoleoncat.com/stats/linkedin-users-in-israel/
- **Date:** 2026-09
- **Confidence:** medium (estimates from ad-audience data, not unique people)
- **Changes my mind:** LinkedIn's own figures for Israel differing widely.

## Handoff notes

- **Revenue lines (speculation, for the merger and the `position` lane):** for a practice this size the likely 2–4 lines are (1) fixed-price brand or personal-branding projects, (2) hourly or hour-bank strategy consulting, (3) talks and workshops sold to organizations, and (4) courses as a minor line. Retainers and cohort courses look less common (C-industry-01, -02, -03, -05). Whether the client sells all four is for `position`.
- **Design execution is cheap in Israel:** Midrag (undated, https://www.midrag.co.il/Content/Tip/13728) lists a business logo at ₪500–2,000 and a graphic designer at about ₪150 an hour. Not minted as a claim because it is undated, but it suggests strategy has to be sold as the value and design as part of it.
- **Climate reading:** shrinking SMB and nonprofit budgets (C-industry-06 to -08) suggest smaller entry offers. That is speculation; complaint is not willingness to pay.
- **Purpose framing for the site copy (for direction, not a fact):** C-industry-09 and -10 point to purpose stated as concrete benefit to the client ("change my world") with proof, not generic green or "doing good" wording. C-industry-11 matters only if the client's own clients sell to EU consumers.
- **The `players` lane** should check whether Israeli peers publish prices. The pattern found here is talks priced on request and courses with a list price (C-industry-03, -05).
- **For the skeptic:** C-industry-02 and -03 each rest on one provider. C-industry-12 and -13 are old. C-industry-09 is a secondary source.
