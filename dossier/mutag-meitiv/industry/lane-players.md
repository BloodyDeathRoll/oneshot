# lane-players.md — the competitive set for מותג מיטיב

## Summary

The Israeli set a would-be client compares with has three groups: nonprofit marketing agencies that sell execution (TikTek, Shepaz), branding studios that list nonprofits as one sector among many (Be Brand, Studio Josef, Tali Gilad) plus a single purpose-only studio (DoGood Studio), and personal-branding coaches and course sellers who work apart from design studios (Shelly Sever, Galit Sharon, Reuta, Rinat Hadar). The crowded claims are "branding + website for nonprofits", "we know nonprofits", "strategy, not just design" and proof by client-logo lists. The rare ones are personal branding and strategic brand planning sold together for purpose-driven owners, a named purpose-branding method with a certification (Brand the Change: the only trainer listed for Israel is the client's founder), an award in the nonprofit category, a podcast run by a studio, and any visible studio price.

## Claims

### C-players-01 — Israeli players that focus only on nonprofits are marketing agencies that sell execution (content, campaigns, video, fundraising), not brand strategy
- **Evidence:** TikTek calls itself "the complete marketing wrapper for organizations and institutions" ("מעטפת שיווקית" per the site's framing). It lists website, copy, video ("1,000+ videos across 32 countries"), media buying, donor retention, print, influencers and design, with nonprofit clients חיוך עם שחר, בית מתן, חיים בשביל החי, ארוחה עשר. Calcalist describes it as "a 2024 marketing firm for nonprofits only", with a sister fundraising company "מיליונים". Shepaz has served "nonprofits and social organizations since 2011": content, visual identity, social, influencers, PPC, newsletters/WhatsApp, Google Grants, with clients WIZO, Maccabi World Union, Good Food Institute, Hebrew Union College. Shiferon (Shiri Kedar), "מחזון - לביצוע" ("from vision to execution"), sells communications, Google and social ads for nonprofits. Brand strategy is at most one line on each list.
- **Source:** TikTek https://www.tikteck.co.il/ ; Calcalist https://www.calcalist.co.il/article/hk11s4zqsa ; Shepaz https://shepaz.com/digital-for-organizations/ ; Shiferon https://www.shiferon.co.il/npo/
- **Date:** 2026-10 (sites fetched); Calcalist 2024
- **Confidence:** high
- **Changes my mind:** a nonprofit-only Israeli player whose lead offer is brand strategy or positioning work, not campaigns or content.

### C-players-02 — "Branding and websites for nonprofits and businesses" is the most crowded claim among Israeli studios: nonprofits are one sector on a generalist list
- **Evidence:** Studio Josef (Jerusalem): "מיתוג ואסטרטגיה עיצובית לעמותות ועסקים" ("branding and design strategy for nonprofits and businesses"); clients include the Rossing Center next to a tyre shop. Be Brand (Sharon Lerman) lists nonprofits as one of 11 sectors, next to lawyers, real estate and fitness, and promises a "digital presence designed to increase donations and volunteer recruitment". Tali Gilad: "מיתוג אסטרטגי... לארגונים, עמותות, חברות ועסקים" ("strategic branding for organizations, nonprofits, companies and businesses"), and the clients shown are mostly commercial (Guardoc, Cake me happy, Israelibox). Search results for "סטודיו מיתוג לעסקים חברתיים ועמותות" were mostly general studios (Baram, Imagine, Tali Sinay, Asaf Yefet). The ones I opened (Baram, Tali Sinay) have no nonprofit focus.
- **Source:** https://josef.co.il/service/strategy/ ; https://s-lerman.com/website/npo/ ; https://taligilad.co.il/ ; https://studiobaram.co.il/en/ ; https://www.talisinay.com/
- **Date:** 2026-10
- **Confidence:** high
- **Changes my mind:** several Israeli studios found whose whole portfolio and positioning is impact-only (only one found, C-players-03).

### C-players-03 — DoGood Studio is the closest direct competitor: the only Israeli studio found that positions solely on purpose ("organizations with meaning")
- **Evidence:** Headline "מיתוג ודיגיטל לארגונים עם משמעות" ("branding and digital for organizations with meaning"). It works with nonprofits, social initiatives and cultural institutions "from defining identity and language to setting up digital systems" (strategy, design, operations). CTA "בואו נעשה טוב ביחד!" ("let's do good together"). No named clients or awards on the homepage, only a portfolio PDF (2025). Hebrew only.
- **Source:** https://dogood.studio/
- **Date:** 2026-10
- **Confidence:** medium (homepage only; the portfolio PDF was not opened)
- **Changes my mind:** the portfolio shows mainly commercial clients, or a second purpose-only Israeli studio of similar size turns up.

### C-players-04 — Israeli branding studios do not publish prices; the only visible shekel prices in the set are on courses and on single agency line items
- **Evidence:** Be Brand, Studio Josef, Tali Gilad, DoGood, Tali Sinay, Baram, Shiferon and TikTek show no project price. Visible prices: Rinat Hadar's "מקסימום מיתוג" course is ₪4,400 incl. VAT (₪3,900 early bird) for 10 Zoom sessions of 2.5 h, max 10 participants. Mateg's "קורס מקיף למיתוג אישי" is ₪1,690 (marked down from ₪2,490). TikTek's newsletter is 2,500 NIS (in Calcalist, not on its own site). Shepaz gives only a media-spend guide ("small nonprofits can spend ~3,000 NIS/month").
- **Source:** https://course.r-h.studio/ ; https://www.mateg.co.il/%D7%97%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA/https-mategvp4me-canva ; https://www.calcalist.co.il/article/hk11s4zqsa ; https://shepaz.com/digital-for-organizations/ ; studio URLs as in C-players-02/03
- **Date:** 2025-11 (Rinat Hadar cycle #13 starting 02.12.25); 2026-10 (others)
- **Confidence:** high for the studios opened; course prices are as shown on the day
- **Changes my mind:** an Israeli impact or branding studio showing a project or package price.

### C-players-05 — In Israel, personal branding is sold by coaches and trainers outside design studios, framed as career or self-presentation, not as purpose-driven brand strategy
- **Evidence:** Shelly Sever: "מיתוג אישי יותר מהכל זאת אסטרטגיה" ("personal branding is, above all, strategy"). She offers a free 5-video YouTube course (5W method, Ikigai, persona), a podcast ("שלי סבר הפודקאסט"), a workbook and workshops. Galit Sharon: "פיתוח מנהלים ועובדים דרך מיתוג עצמי מנצח" ("developing managers and employees through winning self-branding"). Her course "מפת הזהות המקצועית" covers image, body language and dress code; clients are Bank Hapoalim, Migdal, Isrotel; 1,500+ workshops. Reuta Yosef Tzvi offers 1:1 business mentoring for women (4 × 1.5 h a month plus WhatsApp), "זה לא בשמיים, זה בך" ("it's not in the heavens, it's in you"). None of the three sells a visual identity, and none positions on social or environmental impact.
- **Source:** https://shellysever.com/personal-branding-online-course/ ; https://www.galit-sharon.co.il/personal-branding/ ; https://www.reuta.co.il/personal-escort-route/
- **Date:** 2026-10
- **Confidence:** medium (three coaches opened; the Hebrew personal-branding field is large and a search shows many more workshop sellers)
- **Changes my mind:** an Israeli personal-branding provider who explicitly targets purpose or impact entrepreneurs and also delivers brand identity.

### C-players-06 — Hebrew branding courses mostly teach designers to brand for clients; courses that teach purpose-driven owners to build their own brand are rarer
- **Evidence:** Rinat Hadar's course targets "beginners and experienced designers seeking systematic branding methodology", including pricing your design work ("when you have a method, you can specialize as branding professionals"). Tali Gilad sells "designer mentorship & guidance" and a designers' course (Brandstorming). Search for "קורס מיתוג" brought up designer and DIY courses (studioclass "מעצבים מותג" at ₪3,250, Silhouette Cameo branding at ₪950, curlyblack "TEAMWORK DESIGN"). The owner-facing courses found (Mateg, Shelly Sever) are about personal branding with no impact framing.
- **Source:** https://course.r-h.studio/ ; https://taligilad.co.il/ ; search results for "קורס מיתוג אישי אונליין מחיר" (studioclass.co.il, silhouetteisrael.co.il, curlyblack.com: listing snippets, pages not opened) ; https://shellysever.com/personal-branding-online-course/
- **Date:** 2025-11 to 2026-10
- **Confidence:** medium (studioclass, Silhouette and curlyblack seen only as search snippets)
- **Changes my mind:** a Hebrew course aimed at social entrepreneurs, nonprofit staff or values-led owners building their own brand.

### C-players-07 — A named purpose-branding method with a certification is rare in the Israeli set: Brand the Change lists one trainer for Israel, the client's founder
- **Evidence:** Brand the Change's certified-trainers page lists "Hadas Drachli", Israel, Brand Strategy and Personal Branding, sustainability and SMEs. The client's site names its founder as הדס דרכלי (Hadas Drachli) and says it does strategic planning "through the Brand the Change methodology". The certification is a 6-month online programme. No Israeli competitor I opened cites an external purpose-branding method. Shelly Sever cites general tools (5W, Ikigai); Rinat Hadar sells her own "method".
- **Source:** https://www.brandthechange.org/brand-the-change-certified-trainers ; https://www.mutagmeitiv.com/ ; competitor URLs as in C-players-01 to 05
- **Date:** 2026-10
- **Confidence:** high that she is the only Israel-listed trainer; medium on "no competitor cites a method" (homepages and service pages only)
- **Changes my mind:** another Israel-based BTC trainer, or an Israeli competitor using a comparable named framework (e.g. B Corp consulting, Brand Purpose canvases).

### C-players-08 — Proof in the Israeli set is mostly client-logo lists and Google reviews; awards are rarely shown, and the Israel Brand Award's nonprofit category is a rare credential
- **Evidence:** TikTek and Shepaz prove themselves with lists of nonprofit clients, Galit Sharon with corporate logos, Be Brand with "112+ Google reviews averaging 5.0". None of the Israeli players I opened shows an award. The Israel Brand Award (Brand Academy, Yariv Amit; 2024 at the Tel Aviv Museum; 16 categories, 200+ entries) has a category "עמותות וחברה" ("nonprofits and society"), won in 2024 by GIRAF for the "KIDNAPPED" awareness campaign. The client claims a 1st place in nonprofit branding, and its collaborator Dana Rosso a 2022 "best branding in the nonprofits category". Which award these are was not verified here (handoff).
- **Source:** https://marketing.walla.co.il/item/3706956 ; https://s-lerman.com/website/npo/ ; https://www.galit-sharon.co.il/personal-branding/ ; https://www.tikteck.co.il/ ; https://shepaz.com/digital-for-organizations/ ; https://www.mutagmeitiv.com/
- **Date:** 2024 (award); 2026-10 (sites)
- **Confidence:** medium (brandawards.co.il returned 403, so category history and past winners were not opened)
- **Changes my mind:** several Israeli impact studios that show awards, or the client's "1st place" proves to be from a minor or self-run contest.

### C-players-09 — Content assets: podcasts and newsletters belong to coaches and to the international set; Israeli studios have at most a blog
- **Evidence:** Shelly Sever (podcast, workbook, free YouTube course) and Reuta (podcast, blog, courses, videos) are the Israeli players with content beyond a blog. Studios have a blog at most: Be Brand, Josef, Tali Gilad, Tali Sinay, and Shiferon (a blog). DoGood offers a portfolio PDF only. Fifty & Fifty runs a podcast ("Good with Pixels"), a monthly newsletter ("Fifty Digest") and a blog. Brand the Change sells courses, a guidebook, canvases, a membership and a trainer network. The client's site lists a podcast ("On air", women, business and meaning) and courses.
- **Source:** https://shellysever.com/personal-branding-online-course/ ; https://www.reuta.co.il/personal-escort-route/ ; https://fiftyandfifty.org/nonprofit-branding-agency/ ; https://www.brandthechange.org/about ; https://www.mutagmeitiv.com/ ; studio URLs as above
- **Date:** 2026-10
- **Confidence:** medium (homepage-level reads; a studio may run a newsletter not shown there)
- **Changes my mind:** Israeli impact studios with an active podcast or newsletter.

### C-players-10 — The international aspiration set wins on an exclusive impact focus plus hard proof: quantified outcomes, B Corp certification, named awards, and in one case a published price
- **Evidence:** Fifty & Fifty publishes "nonprofit branding investment typically ranges from $15,000-$50,000" and 8–12-week timelines; it is B Corp, with 4 Webby Awards and an Anthem Award. Briteweb: "Together, we'll do a world of good", B Corp and Living Wage certified, offices in Vancouver, Toronto and New York. Studio J Lorne: "50+ brands", "3X average ROI", "$50M+ total client funding secured post-branding", clients Omidyar Network and Code2040; it sells only a $27 checklist. Sereth: "a catalyst for social change", "Brand stewardship for brands who give a crap", serving nonprofits only. Brand the Change (founded 2014 in Nairobi by Anne Miltenburg) teaches, rather than serving agency clients: "We use the power of brand to amplify solutions to the social and environmental crises we face." Of the five, only Fifty & Fifty publishes a price band.
- **Source:** https://fiftyandfifty.org/nonprofit-branding-agency/ ; https://briteweb.com/ ; https://www.jlorne.studio/ ; https://www.serethdesign.com/ ; https://www.brandthechange.org/about
- **Date:** 2026-10
- **Confidence:** high
- **Changes my mind:** n/a for the facts; the "aspiration" framing fails if Israeli clients never look abroad (a matter for the `audience` lane).

### C-players-11 — Hebrew-only sites are the norm for small Israeli players; English versions appear with agencies that court donors or international clients
- **Evidence:** Hebrew only: TikTek, DoGood, Tali Gilad, Tali Sinay, Reuta, Rinat Hadar's course, Mateg. Hebrew plus English: Be Brand (Hebrew and English versions), Shepaz (Hebrew and English; international clients such as WIZO and Maccabi World Union). Josef, Shiferon and Galit Sharon show English navigation elements only. All five international players are English only. The client's choice of Hebrew only (brief answer 11) matches its peer group.
- **Source:** player URLs as above; internal: brief (answer 11)
- **Date:** 2026-10
- **Confidence:** medium (language read from the pages fetched; language switchers were not tested)
- **Changes my mind:** most of the impact-focused Israeli players turn out to run full English sites.

## Handoff notes

- **Crowded (every player says it):** "branding + website for nonprofits and businesses" (C-players-02), "we know nonprofits / the sector's DNA" (01), "strategy, not just design" (02, 05), proof by client logos or Google reviews (08), Hebrew-only sites for small players (11).
- **Rare in this set:** personal branding and strategic brand planning from one provider, for purpose-driven owners (05 + 02 + 03 suggest no one else combines them); a named, certified purpose-branding method (07, Israel-unique per BTC's list); an award in a nonprofit branding category (08); a studio that runs a podcast and courses (09); any visible studio price (04).
- **Direct competitors to watch:** DoGood Studio (same purpose positioning, organizations not people) and Tali Gilad (strategy + designer courses + social-work background). Coaches (Shelly Sever, Galit Sharon, Reuta) compete only on the personal-branding service.
- For `position`: verify which award the client's "1st place, branding for non-profit organizations" is. If it is the Israel Brand Award "עמותות וחברה" category, it is the strongest rare proof in the set. Dana Rosso, the client's collaborator, is a graduate of the client's own course "מיתוג מיטיב" (search summary only, not opened).
- For `sites`: Fifty & Fifty (published price band, podcast + newsletter) and J Lorne (quantified outcomes) are the clearest models for a proof-led page. Whether a shekel price or "from ₪" line helps is a client question (brief answer 13 allows prices in ILS where shown).
- Speculation: the Hebrew search field for "מיתוג אישי" is saturated with workshop sellers, so a site that says "personal branding" without the purpose angle will blend in. The research suggests leading with the combination and the method.
