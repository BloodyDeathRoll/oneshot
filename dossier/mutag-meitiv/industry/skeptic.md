### Ignored competitors / alternatives
- **The sites sample is large agencies** (Koto, Wolff Olins, Collins, Imaginet…). The client is a one-founder practice with courses, a podcast and lectures. The buyer of personal branding compares it with coach and solo sites (Shelly Sever, Reuta, Galit Sharon) and with DoGood and Tali Gilad (players lane). None of these was looked at as a *site*: first screen, CTA, how courses and podcast are integrated. C-sites-04/05 "what converts" may not transfer to a solo practice. This is the reopen below.
- **The players lane misses larger Israeli studios** that show awards and newsletters (NotFromHere, Butcher, iDeez from the sites lane), which narrows C-players-08 and -09.
- **DIY and free alternatives are thin in the research:**
  - Canva and AI logo tools appear only through the Figma survey of product designers (C-industry-14).
  - Free state and accelerator help (C-industry-04, C-audience-10).
  - Cheap Israeli design execution (Midrag, only in handoff notes, undated).
- **Brand the Change sells its own online courses** directly in English (C-players-10). An Israeli owner could take BTC's course instead of the client's Hebrew one. Not examined.
- **Brand Academy runs both a course shop (C-industry-03) and the Israel Brand Award (C-players-08).** It is a competitor on courses and possibly the award's organiser (C-position-07). Not connected by any lane.

### Pain vs willingness to pay
- **Strain is not demand.** C-industry-06/07/08 (closures, the self-employed down about 50 %, 71 % of nonprofits in difficulty) and C-audience-05 (career change) are pain or climate. None shows anyone buying branding.
- **WTP evidence is only list prices and income caps:**
  - list prices: C-industry-02/03, C-players-04, C-position-04;
  - income caps and free alternatives: C-audience-06, -10.
  - No sales, enrolments or conversions were seen for any player, including the client: its course pages are closed or "ended" (C-position-05), and Apple shows 0 ratings (C-position-09).
- **C-industry-11 (EU green claims)** is real law, but no claim shows the client's buyers sell B2C into the EU. It is context, not a need.
- **Every "converts" statement** in the sites lane is the structure of admired sites, not conversion data (the lane says so).
