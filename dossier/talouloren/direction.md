## The brief, in our words

TalouLoren is Tal Oren's brand business, relaunched as a studio of two named partners: Tal (story, strategy, mentoring, the BRAND YOURSELF course and its ten-step method) and Shahar Naor (identity, web, product, motion, AI). The new site is a parallel venue beside talouloren.com, not its replacement (Shahar, 2026-11-02, A11); the old site stays up and its URLs are not redirected. The tagline "Success is Beautiful" stays in English in both languages; the brand idea is "flip the spotlight": the glare that freezes the owner turns to light them.

The site has one job: get a qualified owner-led business into the free Focused Check (בדיקה ממוקדת) or a booked, paid Clarity Session (פגישת ייעוץ, A12). Every page ends in one of the two. Behind them sit Story Sprint, Launch Build, the flagship Partnership and the course. Larger brands get `/brands` and a form. Hebrew first and fully RTL, English a full second language written to match. The voice is warm, calm, confident and direct: no hype, no multiples, no exclamation marks, plural address, Tal's "you are not alone" warmth kept and the 2020 sales register dropped.

The look (A6): dark, heavily black and white, touches of one leading colour, super high end; the partners like the look of today's talouloren.com and shaharnaor.com. The eight sites Shahar named (A7) set the bar for motion and finish. Copy is written fresh in Hebrew, then English (A5). Booking, payment, forms and the Check backend are mocked up only until the partners pick a concept (A10). Videos are the main proof (brief "Media assets", A8). Logos on the proof wall are cleared (A4).

## Positioning

- **Two named partners as the product, not a studio's output.** The reference sites that sell people put faces with names and titles on the first screen; the ones that sell work do not. TalouLoren sells the two of them, so the partners belong near the top, the big names after. (C-references-24, C-references-06, C-references-05)
- **Big-name proof, owner-led promise.** The proof is organisational (Amdocs, Maccabi, Seat, Park Bavli, a 36-logo wall, NBC through Shahar's *Pale Force* animation, Coca-Cola Israel) while the buyer is an owner-led business. The proof says "they have done this at scale"; the copy says "and they will sit with you". (C-position-08, C-position-17, C-position-20; brief "Confirmed cases")
- **A free branding diagnostic is still rare in Hebrew.** The Hebrew diagnostics found were in general business consulting (RND, Vazana), none in branding. The Focused Check can own that step. (C-references-11, C-references-18)
- **Quiet where the category is loud.** The Hebrew coach-site register (countdowns, "last chance", outcome percentages, fear-labelled scores, "8-figure") is exactly what the brief drops, and what Tal's own old site did (1000x lines, "the ultimate warrior"). The calm, high-end look is the differentiator, not only a taste. (C-references-27, C-position-11, C-position-13)
- **A name with no search footprint yet.** "talouloren" has no autocomplete and "טל אורן" suggests other people, so the titles must carry the category words, not the name. (C-position-18, C-position-21)

## Audience

- **First screen and Check: the owner-led business** (solo, new, 2–20 people, SMB, personal brand), as the brief sets them. No audience research in this run (A1).
- **Their search words need qualifiers.** Bare "מיתוג אישי" means party goods in Hebrew search; use "מיתוג אישי לעסק", "בניית מותג אישי", "מיתוג לעסק חדש", "מיתוג לעסקים קטנים", "מנטור עסקי", "אסטרטגיה שיווקית לעסק". "ליווי עסקי" gets no autocomplete. (C-position-21, C-position-22)
- **"Free" is a word they type.** "ייעוץ עסקי לעסקים קטנים בחינם" (free business consulting for small businesses) is suggested; the free Check answers it. (C-position-22)
- **Larger brands and teams:** `/brands` and a form, as the brief says; Tal's channel already holds the films (Maccabi, Amdocs, Bezeq, Clalit, SEAT) that speak to them. (C-position-17)
- **Tal's existing audience** (Instagram 11K, YouTube 187 videos, course buyers) knows the gold; continuity there is the reason the brief suggests it. (C-position-14, C-position-17)

## Page-archetype hints

For the creative-director (station 4d). Hints, not a sitemap; the brief's sitemap and Home order stand.

- **Calm:** Ritz and Ashcroft structure. Serif display, white-on-near-black with one light band, the partner strip in the hero, section labels on a side rail (left in RTL). Motion: reveals and stepped section colour only. Lightest of the three. (C-references-02, C-references-03, C-references-06)
- **Interactive:** Ninia's clarity with Steelwave's motion. A cycling last word in the hero line, the five pains as a scroll-lit list, the promise ladder as outline words filling in turn, the Check's first question answerable on Home. (C-references-05, C-references-07, C-references-11)
- **Unique:** the spotlight as the one big idea: an Igloo-like light field or frost behind the hero as a small shader or a poster loop, Briony's cut-out portraits rising through the "flip" statement, an Amuse-Bouche story order (film → belief → giant list of the two doors → case → "work with us" marquee). No preloader: it delays the Check button. (C-references-01, C-references-04, C-references-08)
- **Check and snapshot:** RND's mechanics (one question, option cards, counter, gate at the end, score page with one next step) in TalouLoren's voice and look. (C-references-11)
- **Work:** big names as named rows with one line each (Porto Rocha, Order), filter tabs above. (C-references-23)
- **Do not:** canvas-only text, 10–30 MB first loads, countdowns, fear-labelled scores, budget questions framed as qualification gates, a cookie wall over the first screen. (C-references-01, C-references-10, C-references-27)
