# How to edit your website — {{business_name}}

You can do this on a phone or a computer. You cannot break the design: you only change words, numbers and photos.

Every change goes live about one minute after you press **Save**.

## Log in

1. Open **{{admin_url}}**
2. Type your email: {{owner_email}}
3. Type your password. Press **Login**.

![Login screen](screenshots/01-login.png)

If you type a wrong password several times, the editor locks for a few minutes. Wait, then try again.

## Change a price

1. Press **{{label_site_content}}**.
2. Go down to **{{label_prices}}**.
3. Press the row you want to change.
4. Type the new price in **{{label_price}}**. Type numbers only.
5. Press **Save**.
6. Wait one minute. Open {{site_url}} and check the price.

![Price list in the editor](screenshots/02-prices.png)

To add a new row, press **{{label_add_row}}**. To remove a row, press the **⋯** next to it, then **{{label_remove}}**.

## Change opening hours

1. Press **{{label_site_content}}**.
2. Go to **{{label_hours}}**.
3. Change the times for each day. For a closed day, switch on **{{label_closed}}**.
4. Press **Save**.

![Opening hours in the editor](screenshots/03-hours.png)

## Change a photo

1. Press **{{label_site_content}}**, then **{{label_photos}}**.
2. Press the photo you want to replace, then **{{label_upload_new}}**.
3. Choose a photo from your phone or computer. Any size is fine — the website makes it the right size.
4. Write a few words in **{{label_alt_text}}** that say what is in the photo. Blind visitors hear these words, and Google reads them.
5. Press **Save**.

![Photos in the editor](screenshots/04-photos.png)

Use only photos that are yours, or that you have permission to use.

## Change text, phone number, WhatsApp, booking link

Same way: **{{label_site_content}}**, find the field, change it, **Save**.

Check the WhatsApp number twice. If it is wrong, customers cannot reach you.

## Two languages

Your site is in {{languages}}. At the top of the editor there is a language switch. Change the text in **each** language. Prices, hours and photos are shared — change them once.

![Language switch](screenshots/05-language.png)

## Change your password

1. Press your name or the round icon, then **{{label_account}}**.
2. Press **{{label_change_password}}**. Type a new password two times.
3. Press **Save**.

## If the change does not show

1. Wait two minutes. Reload the page.
2. Open the site in a private window.
3. Still old? See `06-if-it-breaks.md`.
