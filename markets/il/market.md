# markets/il/market.md — Israel: language, money, domains, pitch.

The market pack for runs with `market: il` (proposal 2026-09-globalsurf-02/004). Facts below come
from the proposal and Shahar's answers; lines marked OPEN are not decided yet.

- **Site language:** Hebrew (`run.yaml` `languages`); a second language only if the brief asks.
- **Script direction:** right to left. Layouts, type, icons with direction, and forms are built RTL;
  numbers, Latin brand names and URLs stay LTR inside RTL text.
- **Pitch language:** Hebrew (Shahar, 2026-10-17).
- **Price on the pitch:** none for the inbound one-off (Shahar, 2026-10-17).
- **Currency:** ILS when a price is ever shown; no price list for this market yet.
- **Domains:** OPEN — check whether Cloudflare Registrar sells the client's TLD (`.co.il` / `.il`)
  before offering to register one; an existing domain is connected after a sale.
