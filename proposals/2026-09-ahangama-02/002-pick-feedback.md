## What this says

1. **Both picks are the interactive variant.** Two out of two. One run is not a pattern, but it is the
   first data point for the designer eval PLAN.md §5 asks for ("which variant humans pick and why").
   Worth watching: if calm keeps losing, `custom.md`'s three-variant brief may be spending a third of
   every station on a variant nobody wants.
2. **Movement is wanted, and more of it than the interactive variant currently has.** Both picks are
   the one with motion, and the Layday note explicitly asks for more microanimation. The unique
   variant's *motion* was liked even where its styling was not — *"nice movement when we have it"*.
3. **Colour tone is a real weakness, separate from colour separation.** *"Good color separation, bad
   color tones."* The grounds are distinguishable from each other; the hues themselves are wrong.
   Layday's palette was taken literally off the hostel's own ochre walls, which is defensible as
   sourcing and evidently not enough as design. This is the first concrete brief for a palette pass.
4. **Unique read as "a little outdated."** Worth understanding before the next run: the devices in it
   (ruled hairline cells, big display type, a colour field) are current, so the dated impression is
   probably coming from the tones — see 3 — rather than the structure.
5. **KA failed at the archetype, not at the layout.** All three were the kitchen noticeboard, so
   rejecting all three rejects the archetype. The station-4d rule that produced it still binds: KA's
   best photograph is 576 × 768 px and cannot be used full-bleed. Whatever replaces the noticeboard
   has to solve the same problem a different way.
