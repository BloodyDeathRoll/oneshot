# 003 — custom.md proposal — designer taste: colour, type, and the three-variant brief

**custom.md proposal.** `agents/designer/custom.md` is Shahar's file. This is suggested text, not a
diff, and nothing in this bundle touches that file.

## Evidence

- `custom.md` describes layout and motion. It says nothing about colour or type. His note at the
  pick gate, "good color separation, bad color tones", had no file to land in.
- His own work is consistent and is the opposite of what the pipeline produced: saturated pure
  blue, cool whites and light greys, clean geometric sans, precise black line, one flat colour
  hit. No cream, no editorial serif, no muted petrol.
- Two picks out of two were the interactive variant. Calm has never been picked. "Conformist
  standard" asks for a template and gets one.

## Suggested paragraph — to be rewritten in his own words

```
Colour: cool, clean and saturated. Cool whites and light greys for grounds, never cream, beige or
ochre. One strong pure colour carried large, taken from the place itself. One flat colour hit
reserved for booking. No muted, dusty or earthy tones unless the business itself is that colour.
Type: clean geometric sans, light weights at large sizes, precise spacing. No decorative serifs.
Line: thin, precise, black. Nothing soft, no gradients as decoration, no drop shadows.
Minimal, precise, extreme: one bold idea per page, everything around it quiet.
Movement: the page answers the visitor — the pointer, the scroll, a tap. One orchestrated moment,
not a fade on every section.
```

## The three-variant brief — three options

a. Keep three, redefine calm: "quiet, not conformist: the same ambition with almost no motion".
b. Three different ideas, all at full ambition.
c. One idea, iterated with the look loop, and the pick gate becomes approve / another round. This
   is what actually produced the gold page.

If the brief changes, the variant table in `agents/creative-director/base.md` §4 follows it.
