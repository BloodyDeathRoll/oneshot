## Lines that conflict with your feedback

- *"Type: clean geometric sans, light weights at large sizes… No decorative or editorial serifs."*
  You asked for tasteful readable text plus handwritten script titles, as on globalsurf.com.
- *"Line and surface: thin, precise, black."* You asked for no excessive lines, borders or underlines.
- *"…overlapping kinetic texts…"* and *"Minimal, precise, extreme"*. Produced giant display words over
  video and near-empty screens, which you rejected.

## Suggested replacement (whole file)

> Lead with the business's own video and best photography: full-bleed, clear, unobstructed. On the
> first screen, only the header, at most one short centred title, and one short call to action.
>
> Colour: clean and bright, taken from the place. One strong booking colour, always with white text on
> it. Grounds are clean whites and light neutrals.
>
> Type: a readable, tasteful text face for everything people read, plus one expressive display face
> (a handwritten script is welcome) for section titles only. Never more than two families. Consistent
> through the whole site, booking included.
>
> Structure: distinct sections, each designed as a moment: rooms sold with galleries, levels with
> their footage, guests' words, the Instagram feed, a proper footer. Slideshows with dot navigation.
> Compact details. Buttons for actions, never underlined text.
>
> Surface: no decorative lines, rules or borders; separate with space, colour and imagery. Soft is
> fine when it is tasteful: rounded corners on cards and buttons, gentle shadows where they help.
>
> Motion: the video carries the movement. Small, useful microinteractions; no reveal animations on
> headings, no effects laid over the footage.
>
> Booking lives inside the site and looks like it: choose the level, see what is included, see the
> rooms, pick dates, then hand off for guest details and payment.
>
> For each website, make three mockups that all meet this bar and differ in personality: one quiet,
> one richer in interaction, one bolder. None is a template, and none is weaker than the business's
> current site.
