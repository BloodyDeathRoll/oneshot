# 002 — A rating in the hero is the agents' choice, not a rule

**Agents:** creative-director 0.4.0 → 0.4.1 (base.md). **Playbook:** 0.6.5 → 0.6.6 (§3 item 1). **References:** the 2026-09-29 calibration line in designer, creative-director, visual-critic and experience-critic `references.md`. **Schema:** `schemas/audit.md` (`recommended.above_fold` wording only).
**Status:** asked for by Shahar in session, 2026-10-17. Applied with this file.

## Why

Two rules disagreed. Playbook §3 item 1 required a proof point (rating with count and source) on the first screen. The 2026-09-29 calibration said "No rating in the hero", as a hard bar for every page.

Shahar, 2026-10-17: that feedback was about Global Surf's harvested Booking.com rating, which belongs in its own section with the visitor testimonials. It is not a rule: a hero rating works for KA Surf and not for Global Surf. The agents decide per business, and the human judges it at the gate.

## Changes

- **playbook §3 item 1:** the first screen needs the media, what + where and the booking action. A proof point there is optional; the concept decides whether the rating sits in the hero or with the reviews.
- **creative-director base.md, "The first screen":** each concept says where its rating sits and why.
- **references.md calibration (4 agents):** "No rating in the hero" becomes "a per-business choice, not a rule", with Shahar's reason. The tagline + rating + button stack over the video stays a hard bar.
- **schemas/audit.md:** the first screen needs "optionally one proof point".

The critics no longer mark a page down for a rating in the hero, or for one missing from it.

## Not changed

- Run files are not edited; the next run pins creative-director 0.4.1 and playbook 0.6.6.
- Global Surf v2 is unchanged: its rating already sits with the reviews.
