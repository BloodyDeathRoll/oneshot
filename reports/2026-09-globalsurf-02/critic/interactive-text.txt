Footwear is discouraged. Chilling out is inescapable.

Surf, breakfast, surf again

Head out with your group for a morning surf. Back at the lodge, a big, healthy breakfast is waiting.

After that the day is yours: relax at the lodge, or go out and explore before the afternoon surf. The evening ends with dinner, together with the other guests.

Twelve rooms, one big family

There are just twelve rooms. That keeps a stay here personal, part of one big happy family. Every room has air conditioning, a ceiling fan, a safe box, a private toilet and shower, a socket next to each bed and, of course, a board rack.

When you're not on the water, lie out on a sunbed by the pool under the palms, hang out in the common area, take an ice-cold drink from the bar, or eat Sri Lankan produce from the lodge kitchen.
