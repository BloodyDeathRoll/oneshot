Shots: `sites/mutag-meitiv/mockups/calm/shots/` (build 25117ba10d00472e, shared set; 13.1k px desktop, 16.1k px phone). Current site: `reports/2026-10-il-brand-01/current-site/shots/` (same as round 1). The dark greens stay by decision (brief 6).

### Three-second test
- **1440 (desktop-00):** calm, warm, on-brand. Hadas looks straight into the lens, in a green shirt against a pine forest that echoes the greens. The ghost "m" is gone, so the right half reads as clean air, not a smudge. One mint button. Better than round 1.
- **390 (mobile-00):** still the strongest screen. Portrait, headline on one line, full-width button, all above the fold. Downside: img-013 is an arm-length selfie with a white lanyard and heavy phone smoothing. It is warm, but less "professionally designed" than round 1's img-034.

### Checklist (evidence)
- **Media:** this is now a real improvement.
  - The method has a sticky 4:5 frame with the icon-curve corner that changes photo per stage: ring canvas with sticky notes, then the cards, then the collage table, then Hadas on the THE CHANGE stage (desktop-04, 05).
  - A full-bleed overhead band of the workshop table (img-007, desktop-05/06) gives the page its first big image moment mid-scroll.
  - vid-004 now sits as a 232 px tile overlapping a ~440 px table photo (img-030), sticky beside the offer lists (desktop-06/07). It finally reads as a composed pair.
  - The podcast band carries the show's artwork (desktop-12). The founder card uses img-034 and the banded img-001 is gone (desktop-10).
  - Still weak: 2 of the 3 blog covers are green tiles with a white icon, cropped at the top (desktop-13). In a "latest posts" row this reads as "missing image" twice. The full-bleed band comes from a 1600 px source drawn at 1440 css px, so it is slightly soft on 2× screens.
- **Composition and grid:** the six card grids are now two (entry cards, team), plus compact list rows for the offers (desktop-06/07: round WhatsApp button, price, title, two-line audience). The sticky photo/list split is used twice: method and offers. Pace varies: green hero → white proof → dark entry cards → split service blocks → motto → sticky method → full-bleed band → split offers → slider → green case-study tile → logos → team → dark podcast → blog → Instagram → closing. Good.
- **Typography:** unchanged and good: Heebo 400, 18–20 px, generous leading. Stage labels are now 15 px. Section headings are still all the same 44 px teal (desktop-01, 04, 06, 07, 13), so there is no display moment besides the hero title and the motto. Inactive stages in the method sit at 42 % opacity on #EAEAE8 (desktop-05, stages 2–3). That is an intended effect, but the body text of a dim stage drops to roughly 2:1 and is hard to read while it is dim.
- **Colour:** mint is back to buttons only, plus mint headings on the dark entry cards (desktop-01). That is acceptable, but the entry cards no longer show any click affordance (no arrow, no hover hint in the still). The dark-green entry cards echo the current site's four blocks, which is good for recognition.
- **Hierarchy and rhythm:** the eye knows where to go, and every screen has one obvious action. It is still long (13.1k desktop) but far less dense per screen.
- **Polish:** the slider edge shadows are gone (desktop-08/09). The quote marks sit inside the cards. The case-study placeholder is replaced by a clean green tile (desktop-09). Remaining nits:
  - The blog-tile icon is clipped at the top edge (desktop-13).
  - On phone, over the dark footer, a sliver of the pill header's shadow and the edge of a mint button show behind the floating header (mobile-21/22, top).
  - On phone, the dim stages read as disabled.
- **Distinctiveness:** better. The icon-curve frame now appears in hero, method, closing and team, and the sticky method with Hadas's own canvas photos belongs to this business. It still reads as a refined version of her current site rather than a signature piece, which is exactly what the calm brief and brief 17 ask for.

### Score: **8 / 10 — PASS (at the bar)**
Clearly professional, pleasant and clearly better than the current site, with two real set-pieces now (the sticky method and the table band with video). It is not yet beautiful enough to sit above the bar. Closest reference: Global Surf gold in discipline and section-by-section design. It is below the gold in emotional pull, because the gold has a full-bleed hero and type with character, and this page keeps a selfie hero and one uniform heading style.

### Better than the current site?
- **First screen: yes.** `calm/shots/desktop-00` vs `current-site/shots/desktop-00`: a larger, sharper portrait, one labelled action, no badge clutter, no boxed 950 px column.
- **Whole page: yes, clearly.** `calm/shots/desktop-sheet` vs `current-site/shots/desktop-sheet`: the same sections in the same order, now readable and composed, with the method, prices, video and Instagram added. Round 1's two regressions are fixed (podcast artwork) or partly fixed (blog covers: 1 of 3 real).

### Remaining fixes (biggest effect first)
1. **Hero photo:** go back to img-034 (round 1, the yellow jacket by the ring canvas) or another non-selfie. img-013 has a lanyard and phone smoothing, and it is also the photo the creative director gave concept 3 as its hero piece (concepts.md §2), so calm and unique risk opening on the same picture. If img-013 stays, crop above the lanyard.
2. **Blog covers:** two green icon tiles in a row of three read as missing images. Use her own photos as covers for the two posts without a business-owned image (e.g. img-033 or img-011 for the strategy/brand posts), or switch the row to a text-led layout with no image slot. Never show the icon clipped (desktop-13).
3. **Method dim state:** raise inactive stages to ≥ 60 % opacity, or dim only the number and title and keep the body at full ink. On phone, show every stage at full strength (mobile reads as disabled today).
4. **One display moment for section titles:** give the four main section heads (method, הרצאות וסדנאות, צוות, אינסטגרם) a larger size step (~56–64 px) or a small icon-curve accent. Today every heading below the hero is the same 44 px teal.
5. **Small polish:** add a hover/arrow affordance on the dark entry cards. Hide the pill-header shadow/button sliver over the dark footer on phone. Use a sharper source for the full-bleed band if one exists, or cap its drawn width.

### Keep
Everything that moved the score: the sticky method with stage photos, the full-bleed table band, the photo + video pair beside compact offer rows, the flat slider, the green case-study tile, the founder card on img-034, the podcast artwork. Also the hero layout, mint for booking only, Heebo 18–20 px, the labelled mobile bar, the Instagram strip, the closing and the footer.

### One sentence for the pick
The current site, done properly. It is instantly hers, very readable, and now has two real set-pieces (the method and the workshop table), though its selfie hero and uniform headings keep it calm rather than striking.

## Round 1

Run 2026-10-il-brand-01 · 2026-10-18 · Hebrew, RTL. Written as a per-variant file (the three variants were reviewed in parallel; merge into `mutag-meitiv.visual.md` for the ranking).

Shots: `sites/mutag-meitiv/mockups/calm/shots/` (build d5448fc9dd9c8659, 17 desktop, 25 mobile; reused, build unchanged). Calibration applied as briefed: dark-green grounds are not a fail here (brief 6, brand_design); surf-only rules (orange, scripts) not applied; the quality bar applies.

## Three-second test
- **1440 (desktop-00):** calm, clean, on-brand. You see Hadas in the yellow jacket first (500×625 photo with the icon-curve corner), then the white headline. The right half below the button is empty green, and the huge faint "m" bowls behind it read as a smudge more than as the icon. I'd keep scrolling, but nothing excites.
- **390 (mobile-00):** the best screen on the page. Wordmark and menu, a big sharp portrait, the headline on one line, a full-width mint button, all above 844 px. Clear and warm.

## Checklist (evidence)
- **Media:** her best photo (img-034) leads, big and well cropped (desktop-00, mobile-00). After that, nothing on the page is large. The service photos are 570 px split images (desktop-02 img-003, a little soft; desktop-03 img-012). The only video, vid-004, is a 232 px tile stuck at the far end of the workshops heading, with about 600 px of empty white between them (desktop-05). The blog cards have **no images** (desktop-13), but the current site's blog has cover photos (current desktop-04). The podcast band is text plus the icon, while the current site shows the podcast artwork (current desktop-01). The founder card photo img-001 has a **dark translucent band baked into the bottom of the image** (desktop-11, top right; the source `img-001.jpg` has it too). The case study's right half is a grey hatched placeholder, "תמונה לדוגמה" (desktop-08). Of the 2000 px photos (img-033, img-011, img-032, img-c03, img-007), only img-c05 shows up, in the closing. There is no full-bleed photo moment anywhere except the Instagram strip (desktop-14).
- **Composition and grid:** the hero split is balanced on desktop, but the left of the text column is dead space (desktop-00). After the hero the page is one row of white cards after another: 4 tool cards (desktop-01), 4 method cards (desktop-04/05), 6 workshop cards (desktop-05/06), 3 course cards (desktop-07), 4 team cards with full bios (desktop-11/12), 3 blog cards (desktop-13). Six card grids on one home page. The method, the business's signature (the ring canvas), is a plain text row (desktop-04).
- **Typography:** Heebo 400 at 18–20 px with generous leading. It is readable and fixes the "too compressed" complaint, and is clearly better than the old 14 px light text (current desktop-00 cards). Every section heading is the same 44 px teal 600 on white or grey (desktop-01, 04, 05, 07, 08, 10, 13, 14), so there is no scale contrast below the hero. The motto in 300 (desktop-04) is elegant but floats with about 150 px of dead white under it. Stage labels (SENSING…) are 12 px tracked caps, which reads as boilerplate.
- **Colour:** the client's own greens are kept and mint is reserved for booking, so the brand is recognisable at once (desktop-00, footer desktop-16). But mint is overused: nine identical full-width mint "לפרטים בוואטסאפ" bars on the workshop and course cards (desktop-06, 07) turn the booking colour into wallpaper. Contrast is fine.
- **Hierarchy and rhythm:** the alternating white/#EAEAE8 bands give it a steady but flat pace. Nothing changes tempo between the hero and the Instagram strip, and the dark podcast band (desktop-12) is the only break. The home page is long (13.6k px desktop, 19k px mobile) and carries every offer, every bio and every testimonial, which is against brief item 9 ("not overpowering with info").
- **Polish:** these need fixing. The quote mark sits on the card's top edge, half outside it (desktop-02 testimonial). The testimonial slider shows the neighbouring slides' shadows as a stub at the left and right edges (desktop-08 bottom, desktop-09). A translucent full-width halo sits behind the floating pill header over the dark podcast band (desktop-13 top). The photo band on img-001 is described under Media. Otherwise alignment and radii are consistent, the buttons are well made, and there is no overflow at 390 (mobile sheet).
- **Distinctiveness:** low. Apart from the icon-curve photo corner (hero, closing) it reads as a clean, well-set landing-page template. Change the logo and the greens, and it could be any consultant.

## Score: **7 / 10 — FAIL (below 8)**
It is clearly professional and better than the current site, but it is competent and tidy rather than beautiful or exciting. The brief asked for "contemporary pleasant **exciting** design with motion", and this delivers "pleasant". Closest reference: Global Surf (gold). It has the same discipline (one CTA, no lines, a real footer, an Instagram strip), but it falls well below the gold on section-by-section design. Gold makes every section its own moment with large media; this page repeats one card grid.

## Better than the current site?
- **First screen: yes.** `calm/shots/desktop-00` vs `current-site/shots/desktop-00`: the photo is 3× the size, there is one labelled action instead of four seals and an unlabelled header link, readable type, and no boxed 950 px column.
- **Whole page: yes, but narrowly.** `calm/shots/desktop-sheet` vs `current-site/shots/desktop-sheet`: cleaner, readable, and adds the method, prices, testimonials and Instagram. It regresses in two places: the blog loses its cover photos and the podcast loses its artwork.

## Fix list (biggest effect first)
1. **Cut the card monotony and the density of the home page.** (a) Workshops and courses: show the two groups as compact lists or one slider per group with a single WhatsApp button per group, not nine full-width mint bars; keep price and status per row. (b) Team: founder card plus a grid of faces with name and role only; the bios open on tap or live on אודות (desktop-11/12 is roughly 1,500 px of bio text). (c) Testimonials stay as the slider. Aim for well under 10k px on desktop.
2. **Give the page large media moments.** Put at least one full-bleed or near-full-bleed photo band mid-page: img-033 (Hadas at Brand Manager Day, 2000 px) or img-011 (the BRANDS OF CHANGE stage) to open הרצאות וסדנאות. Make the vid-004 tile part of a real split moment: the 232 px clip set against img-c03 or img-007 at about 560 px, centred against the copy, not orphaned at the far edge (desktop-05).
3. **Make the method the page's set-piece.** Replace the plain 4-card text row (desktop-04) with the ring canvas: img-003, img-012 or img-032 large, and the four stages beside it as a numbered vertical list in Hebrew title plus Latin label at a readable 14–16 px. Add a quiet stage-by-stage reveal (the calm-level motion the brief asks for).
4. **Fix the visible defects.** (a) Founder photo img-001: crop above the baked-in dark band, or use img-013 or img-034 for the founder card. (b) Blog cards: restore the cover images the current site has (3:2). (c) Podcast band: add the podcast artwork, or Hadas's portrait, beside the text. (d) Testimonial slider: clip the neighbouring slides' shadows (`overflow: hidden` on the track's viewport). (e) Quote mark: inside the card's padding, and the Hebrew opening mark. (f) Pill-header halo over dark bands. (g) Motto: tighten the whitespace beneath it or set it on its own green panel.
5. **Case study and hero finish.** The hatched "תמונה לדוגמה" frame reads unfinished. Make the demo tile a green text panel with the דוגמה badge and no image half, until real images exist. In the hero, scale the ghost "m" so it is clearly the icon's two bowls (or drop it), and give the empty space under the button a purpose, e.g. the 2-line proof (award + BTC) as small white text there instead of a separate white strip.

## Keep
The split green hero with the icon-curve photo corner, and the mobile first screen exactly as it is. The client's greens and mint used only for booking. Heebo 400 at 18–20 px with real leading. The floating pill header and the labelled mobile bottom bar. The proof strip content. Team portraits on their coloured grounds, cropped alike. The Instagram strip. The closing with img-c05 and its curved corner. The dark footer with pill buttons.

## One sentence for the pick
A faithful, clean, very readable cleanup of the current site that the client will recognise at once, but it is a long run of card grids with almost no large imagery, so it reads as a good template rather than an exciting redesign.
