There are six king rooms. Three are on the ground floor, two with a private patio and one with a private kitchen. The other three have a private balcony, over the garden, the pool or both. The family rooms are four suites and a studio; the studio and three of the suites sleep four. The two-bedroom suite sleeps five.

Each room has a work desk, soundproofing and free WiFi. The family rooms have air conditioning and private bathrooms.

There is no published price list. Rates go by date, and the booking links show what is available.

Among cafés and restaurants

Cafés and restaurants surround the hotel, and there is a fruit stand nearby. Guests score the location 9.6 out of 10 on Booking.com.

Koggala Airport is about 5 km away.

Pool, restaurant, spa

A year-round outdoor pool, at the centre of the hotel.

The outdoor pool is in the middle of the hotel and open all year. The restaurant serves breakfast with local specialities and fresh pastries, plus Chinese, Indian and Italian dishes. There’s a coffee house on site too, and a spa and wellness centre.

For families there are the family rooms and an outdoor play area. Kids’ meals and babysitting cost extra.

Practicalities

Arriving and leaving. The front desk is staffed 24 hours, with private and express check-in. You can check in from 14:00 until midnight and check out at any hour.

Paying. Payment at the hotel is cash only.

Parking and the airport. Parking is free and private, but you need to reserve it. There’s an airport shuttle as well.

During your stay. Housekeeping is daily. There is laundry, dry cleaning and ironing, bicycles to rent, non-smoking rooms, and facilities for guests with disabilities.
