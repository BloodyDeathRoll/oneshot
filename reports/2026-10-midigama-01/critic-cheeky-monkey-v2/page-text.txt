Catch waves, not worries

Cheeky Monkey is a surf and yoga camp on the main road through Midigama, close to the main point breaks.

Right alongside the guesthouse is Baba's Surf Shop. Rent a board there or buy one, and bring yours in if it needs a repair.

Down at the water
The spot
Close to the main point breaks, with many more surf breaks a short walk away.
The season
The best time is from October till March or April. The waves are nice and the beach is clean.

Baba's Surf Shop

There are 400 boards for rent, in different sizes, tails and colours.

If your own board gets damaged, the shaper here can fix it fast.

On the shelves there's beachwear, straps, bags and, of course, wax. It's a shop run by surfers, for surfers.

Up on the roof

The house looks out on a 180° sea view. One guest wrote that "the ocean view is amazing from the rooftop".

Yoga happens up there too, at sunrise and at sunset, facing the ocean.

Three rooms in 2009

Cheeky Monkey opened in late 2009 as a small guesthouse with three rooms, a tiny surf shop and a restaurant. By 2021 it had ten rooms.

Baba founded it. Like most kids in the area, he taught himself to surf at twelve. He worked as a surf instructor too, and the shop carries his name.

Getting here

Cheeky Monkey is on the Galle–Matara main road. Booking.com puts Midigama Beach 350 m away and Midigama train station 800 m, then Ahangama Beach at 2 km, Weligama Beach at 3.3 km and Koggala Airport at 10 km.

Galle - Matara Main Road, Midigama East Midigama, Ahangama 80650, Sri Lanka

Airport drop USD 65 | Airport pick-up USD 65 | Airport drop and pick-up USD 130 | published 2021
