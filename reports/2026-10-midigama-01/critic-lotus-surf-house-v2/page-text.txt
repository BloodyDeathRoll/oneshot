In the garden

The house is in a shady garden on Indhana Road. There's a hammock under the trees, and the ping-pong table has a thatched roof over it.

Every room downstairs opens onto the garden through its own terrace, with chairs outside and a table to eat at.

The garden rooms are downstairs. “The standard rooms may not be the newest,” Yelum says, “but they are popular with many vacationers due to their charm.”

Getting here

Midigama railway station is 50 m from the house, so you can come by train.

Further out, Booking.com lists Ahangama Beach at 1.4 km, Weligama Beach at 4.2 km and Koggala Airport at 9 km.

On Google you may also find the house under its old name, Yelum Guest House.
