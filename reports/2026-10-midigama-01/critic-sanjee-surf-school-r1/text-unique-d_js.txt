Before you book

You need to be able to swim at least 50 meters unaided.

We teach kids from age 6 and up. Anyone under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.

Cash on the day can be LKR, USD or EUR. All prices are as of 5 November 2026.

Board rental

Our shop sits 30 seconds from the sand at Coco Beach, Weligama. Free dry storage there while you eat or rest between sessions.

We rent soft-top foamies 8'0–9'0, mini-mals 7'2–7'10, mid-lengths and funboards 6'8–7'4, fish and hybrids 5'8–6'4, performance shortboards 5'10–6'2 and longboards 9'0–9'4.

Each board comes with a leash sized for it (6 ft – 9 ft) and fins fitted to your style (FCS-II or Futures). You also get wax for tropical water and a fresh wax comb.

To rent a board we need an ID or passport copy and a refundable deposit. Ask us how much it is.

Coco Beach is our home spot, a beach break. Smaller crowds than the main peak and a friendly, mellow vibe, ideal for first pop-ups.

Best surf season runs November to April; peak swell and dry weather are December–February.

Other waves, as we rate them

Jungle Beach and Weligama Main Point are both beach breaks we'd call beginner waves. Mirissa, also a beach break, goes from beginner to intermediate.

For intermediates there's Fishermans (a reef break), Island Point (a point break), Plantation Point (a right-hand reef) and Lazy Left in Midigama, a long left-hand reef. Lazy Right, also in Midigama, is a right-hand reef that goes from intermediate to advanced.

Ram's Right, a reef break, and Kabalana (The Rock), an A-frame reef, are for advanced surfers.

By tuk-tuk

The Surf Spot Safari, the Full Coast Adventure and the Sunrise Session are the three tours that go looking for waves.

The other nine tours are outings along the coast and inland, at estimated introductory prices, with pickup and return within Weligama.

Those outings don't include meals or activities.

A tour's price is for the tour, not per person. Ask us how many people can come.
