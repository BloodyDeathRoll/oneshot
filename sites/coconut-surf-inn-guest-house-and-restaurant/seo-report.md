The 4g build already had the search plumbing of the other finals in `final/src/lib/site.mjs`: the IS_PREVIEW rule, `seoHead`, `businessLd` with `geo` (via `gaps.geo`), robots, sitemap and llms.txt, and the JSON-LD block at the end of body. ## Still wrong, and what it would take

1. **The name is split three ways on Google.** There is the inn's listing ("Coconut Surf Inn Guest House and Restaurant", 4.8 · 88) and the restaurant's own unclaimed listing ("Coconut Surf Restaurant (2nd floor)", 4.6 · 39, house number 44 against Booking's 49). There is also the "Coconut Beach" place, which carries the inn's phone. The owner should claim the restaurant listing and remove the phone from the beach place. `alternateName` covers the first on our side. This is a pitch point.
2. **The title has no category word.** "Coconut Surf Inn, Weligama" follows the house rule. "Guesthouse" and "restaurant" appear only in the description. A per-locale `seo.title` (proposal A) would let the owner write, for example, "Coconut Surf Inn, Weligama | Guesthouse by the beach".
3. **The restaurant is not marked up as its own entity.** It is described in a copy block and in `hoursNote`, but there is no `Restaurant` / `FoodEstablishment` with `openingHoursSpecification` and no menu. The schema has no restaurant module (business-types.md "Open"), so this was not added.
4. **`alternateName` and Tripadvisor live in `gaps.json`, not in the schema** (proposals B and C). They are seeded from the dossier and the owner cannot edit them.
5. **No production domain yet.** Canonical, og:url, og:image, the JSON-LD `@id` / `url` / `image` and the sitemap appear only with `SITE_URL=https://<domain>/`.
6. **og:image is WebP.** A JPEG for older scrapers would need a new shape in `scripts/photos.mjs`. None was made.
7. **No favicon.** The dossier has no logo file.
8. **No price or `priceRange`.** Rates are live Booking.com inventory (by design).
9. **`rooms/` CLS is 0.0386 on first load.** That is under 0.1, and it was the same at 4g. The h1 is its LCP, so it is probably the Caveat swap. I left it because fixing it would mean a visual or font change.

## Refused or omitted

- **`aggregateRating` / `review`:** omitted. The ratings are real (Booking 8.5/10 · 142, Google 4.8/5 · 88, Airbnb 4.44/5 · 54, read 2026-11-04), but Shahar's no-ratings-markup decision applies to every site.
- **"Coconut Beach" 4.8 / 120:** this is the beach, not the inn. It is never used.
- **Kayak / Momondo in `sameAs`:** they are meta-search mirrors of Booking.com, not listings of the business.
- **The restaurant's Google listing in `sameAs`:** it is a separate entity with a conflicting house number.
- **Tripadvisor's 5/5 · 4:** it is read only off Google's panel (Tripadvisor returned 403). It is not shown or marked up.
- **"Surf" claims beyond the name, the break and the season:** the inn sells no lessons or rentals, so there is no `SportsActivityLocation`.
- **`numberOfRooms`:** dossier `room_count` is in no content field.
