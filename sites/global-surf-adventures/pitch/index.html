What you will be able to change yourself

Today the preview is a finished build, with no login yet. With the managed or the self-hosted option below, you get your own login to a simple admin page that shows only the lodge. You fill in fields, not code, so an edit can't break the design. Press Publish, and the site rebuilds itself.

  • Basics the lodge's name, the tagline, the closing line
  • Text the welcome, the week, the rooms introduction, getting here
  • Photos and video the hero video and its still, the level clips, the gallery photos. Upload from your phone; the site makes the sizes itself.
  • Packages the package names and the "from" price
  • Stay check-in and check-out times
  • Reviews remove any rating or quote; we add new ones with their source
  • Surf the spot text, and the forecast on or off
  • Contact and booking phone, WhatsApp, email, address, map link, and the links into your booking engine

The layout, colours and fonts stay fixed. Some parts of this design have no field yet: the room galleries and descriptions, the level one-liners, the lists of what each package includes, and the booking page's fallback dates and prices. We change those for you when you ask.

How it can run: you choose

Nothing is set up yet: no hosting, no login, no domain. We set it up once you choose. Managed is the one we recommend: we host the site and keep it running, and hosting, updates and backups are our job. You pay a monthly fee that we agree with you, and you edit your content whenever you like. Self-hosted: we install the same site and editor on a server of your own for a one-time fee that we agree with you. It is then fully yours. You pay your server company roughly $6–12 a month, and you take care of updates and backups yourself. Files only: if you never want to edit it yourself, we hand you the finished site as files that run on any web host; every change then needs a developer.

If you want the site on a domain you already own, we connect it.
