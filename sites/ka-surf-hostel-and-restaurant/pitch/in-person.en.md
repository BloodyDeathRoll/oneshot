# In person — KA Surf Hostel (English)

Show it on your own phone, then hand the phone over. Preview: https://ka-surf-hostel.os1.workers.dev/ (it's a preview address, and search engines are told not to list it).

## Opening line

"Your guests give you 9.8 on Hostelworld, but you don't have a website of your own. So we made you one. Can I show you on my phone?"

## Points (scroll as you talk)

1. **It's yours, in your words.** The welcome comes from your own Hostelworld text. The photos are yours, from Booking.com and Instagram. Your three ratings are there with where and when we read them, and five guest reviews word for word. (Stop on the family quotes.)
2. **Booking works the way it already does.** Tap "Check dates": it opens your Hostelworld, Booking.com or Agoda page. "Call" rings +94 77 374 6053. Nothing new for you to answer.
3. **It's quick on a slow phone.** The first screen is 241 KB and the text shows in about 0.4 seconds, measured on a slowed-down connection.
4. **You change it yourself.** You get your own login. Text, photos, rooms, check-in times, amenities, phone and booking links. In our test a change was live in about 20 seconds. Reviews can be removed but not rewritten.
5. **We left out what we couldn't check.** No prices, WhatsApp, email, restaurant or parking yet. Ask them:
   - Does +94 77 374 6053 take WhatsApp?
   - Is the restaurant still open?
   - Check-in: 13:00–23:00, or 24 hours?
   - Is there parking for guests?
   - AI assistants such as ChatGPT can read and quote the site. Keep that, or block them?

## The offer

"There are two ways to keep it. The usual one: we host it, keep it online and do the updates and backups, and you just edit. That's a monthly fee. Or we put it on a server you rent yourself and hand it over completely. That's a one-time fee, then roughly 6 to 12 dollars a month to your hosting company, and the updates and backups are yours."

If they own a domain name already: "We connect it." Don't offer to buy one.

## The ask

"Have a look at it tonight. If you like it, tell me, or reply to our WhatsApp message, and we'll sit down and go through what you want changed."

## If they ask "How much?"

Don't give a number, because none has been set. Say:

"It depends on which way you choose, hosted by us or on your own server, and on what you want added, like WhatsApp or the restaurant. We'll agree the price with you, and nothing starts until you're happy with it. First tell me: do you like it?"

## Don't

- Don't criticise the listings or the Instagram. The ratings are great, so say that.
- Don't promise more bookings, a domain, or a Google ranking.
- Don't name other hostels.
- Don't rush them. There is no deadline.
