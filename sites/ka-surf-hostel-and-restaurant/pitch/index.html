What a guest finds today

KA Surf Hostel has no website of its own. A guest who looks you up finds your listings on Booking.com, Hostelworld, Agoda, TripAdvisor and Google Maps, and your Instagram. Your guests clearly love the place: 9.8 out of 10 on Hostelworld from 66 reviews, 9.5 on Booking.com from 38, and 4.8 out of 5 on Google from 42 (read on 19 September 2026).

What a guest can't do today:

  • Open one page that is only about you. Every booking link they find goes to a platform that takes a commission on the booking.
  • Read your welcome in one place with your photos. Your best writing is on Hostelworld and your photos are split between Booking.com and Instagram.
  • Be sure they have the right place. Google lists you as "KA surf hostel and restaurant", Hostelworld as "K A Surf Hostel", Booking.com as "KA Surf Hostel".
  • See your Instagram without the app. On a phone browser it asks them to open the app first.

The new site

One page about KA Surf Hostel, in English, built for a guest on a phone. It uses your own words and 11 of your own photos from Booking.com and Instagram. It shows your three ratings with where and when they were read, and five guest reviews quoted word for word. It lists all six room types, from the mixed, female and male dorms to the deluxe doubles.

We left some things off because we could not confirm them. We would rather leave a gap than guess:

  • Your prices. Rates change by date on the booking sites, so the site sends guests there.
  • A WhatsApp number and an email address. We could not find either published anywhere.
  • The restaurant. Google's name says "and restaurant", but we found no menu or opening hours.
  • Parking. Booking.com says there is none, and Hostelworld says it is free.
  • Fibre Wi-Fi. Your Hostelworld page mentions it, but Booking.com guests score the Wi-Fi 2.5 out of 10, so we left it out until we hear from you.

What you can change yourself

You get your own email and password login. It opens your site only. When you save a change, the site rebuilds itself. In our test, a text change was live on the site about 20 seconds after saving. You never touch the design, so an edit can't break the page.

Basics

Your name, your tagline, the languages you speak.

Text

Your welcome, "about us", what you offer, good-to-know notes.

Photos

Add, remove and reorder your photos, up to 24.

Rooms & stay

Your rooms and beds, check-in and check-out times, and a note for guests.

Amenities

The list of what you have: breakfast, lockers, laundry, scooter rental, and so on.

Surf

Your wave in your own words, and the surf forecast for Kabalana, on or off.

Contact & booking

Your phone number, address, map link, and the booking links guests use.

Reviews

Ratings and guest quotes are copied word for word from the platforms. You can remove one, but you can't add or rewrite one, so guests can trust them.

The editor also has Opening hours, Prices, Lessons and Rentals & shop tabs. This design doesn't show those on your page yet, and it doesn't show a WhatsApp or email button yet either. If you want them, tell us.

How you keep it

Two ways, and you choose. We host it for you (what we suggest): the site stays online, you edit it from your login, and hosting, updates and backups are our job, for a monthly fee we agree with you. Or it runs on your own server: we install the same site and editor on a server you rent, check that it works, hand it over and remove our access. That is a one-time fee we agree with you. After that it is fully yours. You pay your hosting company roughly $6–12 a month, and updates and backups are yours to look after.
