# Layday Surf Ahangama: in person, with the preview on a phone

Open https://layday-surf.os1.workers.dev/ on the phone before you walk in. Show it; don't read it out.

**Opening line.** "Your guests love this place: 10 on Hostelworld, 10 on Booking.com, 5 stars on Google. But when someone searches for Layday, there is no website of its own. So we built one from your listings. Can I show you on my phone?"

**Points (scroll as you talk)**

1. **The first screen.** "A two-minute walk from Kabalana", your headline from Hostelworld, your rating, and one button, "Check dates", that opens your Hostelworld, Booking.com and Agoda pages. "Call" rings +94 77 855 3343.
2. **Finding the door.** One guest wrote it was "a little bit difficult to find". The page walks guests through the black gate to the door, with a map link. Point at it.
3. **Your guests' own words.** Five reviews, copied word for word, and all three ratings with the date we read them. Nothing on the page is made up: 84 facts, every one from your listings or reviews.
4. **Fast on a bad connection.** On a slowed-down phone test it opens with 184 KB and shows in 0.39 seconds. No pop-ups, no trackers.
5. **You change it yourself.** Your own login. Text, photos, the room, times, amenities, phone and booking links. Save, and the page updates by itself; on this site it took about 17 seconds. The design stays fixed, so nothing you type can break it.

**The offer.** Two ways. Managed, which we recommend: we host it and keep it running (hosting, updates and backups are our job) and you pay a monthly fee. Or self-hosted: we install it on a server of your own, it is fully yours for a one-time fee, you pay roughly $6–12 a month to the server company, and you look after updates and backups.

**The ask.** "Would you like to keep it? You can also just tell us what to change: the number of beds, your prices, a WhatsApp number."

**If they ask "How much?"** Don't give a number, there isn't one yet. "We agree that with you. It depends on which of the two ways you choose and what you want on the page. Tell us what matters to you and we'll come back with a figure." Then go back to the page.

**Keep in mind**

- It is a preview: Google is told not to list it until they decide. Don't say it is on Google.
- No website address of their own is promised; the link is the preview link.
- Prices, WhatsApp and the surf season are not on the page (they were never published). Showing them needs a change to the design, which we make.
- A guest review names "Rukshan and his family" as the hosts. It is not confirmed by the business: ask for the owner, don't open with the name.
- Nothing about their Booking.com, Hostelworld or Agoda listings changes; the site links to them.
