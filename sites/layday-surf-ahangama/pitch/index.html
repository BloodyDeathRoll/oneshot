Today: what a guest finds

Layday has no website, no Instagram and no Facebook. A guest who searches for it finds three booking listings (Hostelworld, Booking.com and Agoda) and a pin on Google Maps.

The listings carry your own words and photos, and your guests' praise. But some things a guest cannot find there:

  • a page that is Layday's own, with its name, to send to a friend;
  • the way in: the large black gate and the door to the right of the house. That is only written in one guest's review, which says the place was "a little bit difficult to find";
  • a WhatsApp number or an email address, because neither is published anywhere;
  • all three ratings in one place.

What you can change yourself

You get your own login, an email address and a password. It opens your site and no one else's. You change things in a simple form, press save, and the live page updates by itself. When we tested it on this site, a change was live about 17 seconds after saving.

The form has these tabs:

  • Basics
  • Text
  • Opening hours
  • Prices
  • Photos
  • Rooms & stay
  • Lessons
  • Rentals & shop
  • Amenities
  • Surf
  • Reviews
  • Contact & booking
BasicsThe business name and the headline under it.
TextThe words in each part of the page: the intro, "Just one dorm", "Newly built, and kept that way", "The room", "Finding the door" and "Practicalities".
PhotosAdd, remove and reorder photos. Upload them straight from your phone: the site crops, resizes and compresses them. You never have to.
Rooms & stayThe room, its beds and a note; check-in and check-out times; the house rules line.
AmenitiesThe list of facilities (add, remove, add a note like "additional charge").
SurfThe line about the wave, and the Rosa forecast on or off.
Contact & bookingPhone, address, map link, and the booking links behind "Check dates".
ReviewsRatings and guest quotes are copied word for word from the platforms. You can delete one. You cannot edit or add one: we add new ones from the platforms, so what the page quotes is always what guests wrote.

Opening hours, Prices, Lessons and Rentals & shop are in every editor, but this page's design does not show them yet, and neither do the WhatsApp, email and surf season fields. If you want your prices or a WhatsApp button on the page, that is a change we make to the design.

You cannot change the layout, colours or fonts. That is on purpose: whatever you type, the page cannot break.

What we could not find

We only used what is public. These were not, so the page leaves them out rather than guess:

  • A price per night. Your rates only exist as live dates on the platforms, so the page says rates change with the dates and sends guests to your listings.
  • The number of beds. Booking.com lists a 6-bed dormitory; a guest on Hostelworld wrote 4 bunk beds. We did not publish either.
  • A WhatsApp number or email address. None is published, so the page offers your listings and the phone number.
  • Your surf season, and how long Layday has been open.

You can tell us any of these. The number of beds you can type in yourself today (Rooms & stay); prices, WhatsApp and the season need the design change described above.
