seo 0.2.2 · run `2026-10-midigama-01` (test, operator alon) · 2026-11-04 · site: `sites/lotus-surf-house/final/` (the 4g build, one page)

The 4g build already carried most of the search plumbing, ported from cheeky-monkey's 4h into `final/src/lib/site.mjs`: the IS_PREVIEW rule, `seoHead`, `businessLd`, robots, sitemap and llms.txt. ## Still wrong, and what it would take

1. **The brand is split across two Google listings.** "Lotus Surf House" has 4.9/5 from 78 reviews. "Yelum Guest House", the former name, still has its own listing (4.7/5, 13 reviews) with the same phone and address 7 m away, and the Facebook page is "Midigama guesthouse YELUM". Booking.com's old Yelum URL redirects. The fix belongs to the owner: merge the Google listings or mark the old one as closed/moved. On the site side, `alternateName: "Yelum Guest House"` would tie the names together, but no field holds it (proposal B). This is a pitch point.
2. **The h1 is the name only.** "Guesthouse in Midigama" is the next line, a `<p class="where">`. That is the right order for the brand query, which is the one that matters (base.md §6), and the title and description carry the category words. Folding the tagline into the h1 would restyle the hero (h1 is `.script` Parisienne), so it was not done. Decision for Alon if he wants it.
3. **The title has no category word.** "Lotus Surf House, Midigama" (26) follows the house rule. "Guesthouse" appears only in the description. A per-locale `seo.title` field (proposal A) would let the owner write "Lotus Surf House, Midigama | Guesthouse in Sri Lanka" without a hard-coded string.
4. **`sameAs` is only Booking.com and Google.** The dossier also has the Facebook page (old name, last post 2018), two Tripadvisor entities and Kayak/Priceline listings. None of these is in a content field, and the Facebook page carries a different phone number (+94 71 258 5804). That conflict is recorded in the dossier, so none of them was added (proposal C).
5. **`geo` lives in `gaps.json`, not in the schema.** If the owner ever pastes a Google Maps URL with a pin into `contact.mapUrl`, that pin wins. Otherwise the seed's value stands (proposal D).
6. **No production domain yet.** Canonical, og:url, og:image, the JSON-LD `@id` / `url` / `image` and the sitemap appear only with `SITE_URL=https://<domain>/`. On the `workers.dev` preview, link previews show no image (`twitter:card` falls back to `summary`). That is correct for a noindexed preview.
7. **og:image is WebP.** WhatsApp, Facebook and X read it. A JPEG rendition for older scrapers would be a new media file, made by a new shape in `scripts/photos.mjs`; none was made.
8. **No favicon.** The dossier has no logo file, and the wordmark is type.
9. **No price.** Rates are live Booking.com inventory, so there is no `priceRange` and no `makesOffer` (by design, as at 4e/4g).

## Refused or omitted because the sources don't support it (or a decision forbids it)

- **`aggregateRating` / `review`:** omitted. `ratings[]` holds Booking.com 9.0/10 (125) and Google 4.9/5 (78), both real and dated 2026-11-03. Shahar's no-ratings-markup decision (KA), applied to every site since, holds here too.
- **`alternateName`, Facebook / Tripadvisor / Kayak in `sameAs`, a lodging sub-type:** these are in the dossier but in no content field (base.md hard constraint 4). See above.
- **`checkinTime`:** "any hour" is not a time, and `checkInFrom` is null.
- **`numberOfRooms`:** the dossier's `room_count` is not in a content field.
- **"Surf" claims:** the business publishes no surf service (dossier `businessType_note`), so no `SportsActivityLocation` type was added and no surf wording was put in the title or description beyond the name.
- No superlatives or category words beyond the owner's fields. "Superb" (Booking's label) is not used.
- **Description, considered and left:** swapping the Breakfast sentence for `spot` ("Booking.com puts Midigama Beach 250 m from the house.") would carry the beach query. It reads awkwardly in a snippet, so the 4g choice stands. Proposal A's `seo.description` is where the owner would make that call.
