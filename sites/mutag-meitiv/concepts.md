## 3. The page archetype: **The programme** (התוכנייה)

The series-1 archetype was the table where a brand is made, read by an owner. For organisations the home page is read the way a commissioning body reads a conference or training programme:
1. **Who is speaking:** Hadas, on stage, in the brand's greens.
2. **What can be booked:** the four formats, a talk, a team workshop, a strategy or rebrand, professional training (`path`).
3. **How a process runs:** the four stages.
4. **Who has had her:** the award and the judges' words, the bodies she is grateful to, the 2024–2025 talks.
5. **The fringe:** individuals — personal branding, courses, open workshops — then content and contact.

It belongs to this business because her organisational proof literally *is* programmes: the Brand Manager Day 2024 line-up, the Mandel Kfar Blum 2025 workshop and the festival programme 2025. Her own Instagram announces them as posters (ig-04, ig-05, ig-07). The archetype organises the media around the stage and the room instead of the table.

**Nothing is removed (brief 17, 19).** The home page *leads* with organisations; individuals keep their full pages. `/personal-branding`, the open workshops (Brand & Breakfast, THE BETTER 4, the free intro workshop, the community meetups) and the courses are one tap from a compact "גם לבעלי עסקים ולאנשי מקצוע" block, from the menu, and from the phone bar's menu.

**Menu (6 + action):** לארגונים (the home page's path; on inner pages it links back) · תכנון אסטרטגי למותג · הרצאות וסדנאות · מיתוג אישי לעסק · קורסים · אודות · WhatsApp. Content (podcast, blog) is in the footer and as one home row (C-sites-19).

**Shared rules (all three concepts):**
- **Booking.**
  - **Primary action:** WhatsApp, label "כתבו להדס בוואטסאפ". On the home page every general button carries **`path.ctaText`** ("היי הדס, אני פונה בשם ארגון…"); each format row carries its own service's `ctaText`. One tap from any screen. The labelled phone bar appears once the hero button scrolls away, and the header pill hides while the bar shows (L9).
  - **Booking section order** (`audit.json.recommended.above_fold`), on each format's page and in each concept's closing: what_where (the format and who it is for) → included (the 4 stages, or the talk's `format`) → price (only where published: therapists-retreat 580 ₪ + מע"מ, btc-professionals 5200 ₪ + מע"מ; nothing for strategic planning or the talk) → trust (the judges' quote or one testimonial) → WhatsApp.
  - **Calendly** ("שיחת הכרות בזום, ללא תשלום") appears once, in the closing (N9).
- **Type:** Hebrew-designed families, body 18 px / 1.65 on phone, 19–20 px desktop, weight 400, measure ≤ 62 ch (C-sites-10, C-sites-20). Latin only for names and terms (C-sites-11). Each concept's family differs from every series-1 family (Heebo; Assistant + Playpen; Frank Ruhl + Heebo), so none of the six looks like another. Unique keeps Heebo as its text face, but its display face is new.
- **Motion:** RTL forward = left, 100–450 ms, native scroll only. No scroll-jacking, smooth-scroll library, loader, pop-up or overlay widget (references #20; C-sites-12, C-sites-22). Reduced motion: static, all content in place. JS off: everything readable, every link works.
- **Surface:** no lines, borders or underlines; buttons for actions (calibration). Photos in a run share one ratio (house rule 5; references #26). No captions under photographs.
- **Logos (D2, L9):** under "מודה על הזכות להטיב ב:", one crisp deep-ink treatment (`#173B34` on white, or white on deep green). Each logo is at least 40 px tall, at the same optical size, and none is washed out. All 15 are shown in a 5×3 grid (desktop) / 3×5 (phone), or in one row per concept below. Third-party marks are shown as on the client's site (run default already decided).
- **Demo case study (L5):** a designed cover (deep green, the m-icon, the client name, a "דוגמה" badge), never an empty frame on the home page.
- **Instagram strip:** as series 1 (title, line, "@mutagmeitiv" pill, 6 square tiles edge to edge, 3×2 on phone). Tiles: ig-01, ig-08, ig-03, ig-02, ig-09, ig-11; **never ig-06**. ig-04 and ig-05 are already shown beside their talk lines.
- **Team (brief 24):** on the home page, one row of faces (founder larger, then the others; reflowing for 1–20 people), name + role, bios open on tap. The full team with bios lives on אודות. It is never next to the award (D1).
- **Budget:** home first load ≤ 1.5 MB with video excluded; the hero photo AVIF ≤ 300 KB; 2 families ≤ 150 KB, self-hosted and subset; JS ≤ 300 KB (expected < 25 KB); 0 third-party requests on load. Video loads only near the viewport, after first paint, never under reduced motion or Save-Data. vid-004 uses the 464-px renditions series 1 already made (webm 0.99 MB / mp4 1.41 MB).
- **Phone length (L7):** each concept states its budget. The designer measures it and cuts in this order: (1) fewer testimonials shown (3); (2) the blog row to 2 posts; (3) offers behind "הצג הכל".

## 4. Three concepts

### Concept 1 · calm — **"דף ההזמנה" (The commission sheet)**

**The idea in one sentence.** A plain, confident page for whoever has to commission her. Hadas at the conference opens it full-bleed, the four formats follow as a clean numbered-free list, and the judges' words carry the proof. It reads like a well-made speaker-and-facilitator sheet.

**The one thing.** **The green dress at the conference** (img-033): Hadas in the brand's own green, full length, at a branding conference. It is the only loud image. Everything else is quiet type, white and deep green.

**Structure (home), with phone budget.**
1. **Header:** white pill (icon in `#00574C` by CSS mask), menu, WhatsApp. ~64 px.
2. **Hero, stacked: full-bleed photo over a deep-green title band** (details below). ~844 px.
3. **"לעמותות, לארגונים ולצוותים":** `intro` on white, 2 short paragraphs in a 600 px column, beside (desktop) / under (phone) a 4:5 crop of img-ig-01 (Hadas on stage before the BRAND THE CHANGE slide, from the audience). ~900 px.
4. **"מה ארגונים מזמינים":** the 4-item list (D-notes). Each row: a 40 px line icon (template-drawn, one per format: a mic, a group, a compass, a cap), the service `title` in 600, the matching `path.body` sentence (the text after the colon), and a 44 px round WhatsApp button carrying that service's `ctaText`. The whole row also links to the page. Below the list: one mint button "כתבו להדס בוואטסאפ" with `path.ctaText`. On `#EAEAE8`. ~1,100 px.
5. **The award moment, full-bleed on `#173B34`:** the seal img-040 (96 px), the award line (`proof.award`, once, D1), then `judgesQuote` set large (Plex 26 px / 1.6, white, ≤ 34 ch measure, the Hebrew quote marks ״ ״ inside), linked to brandawards.co.il (D3), and the attribution in mint (`#4BD1A0` on `#173B34`, 6.38:1 ✓). ~900 px.
6. **"מודה על הזכות להטיב ב:":** 15 logos on white (D2), then the three talk lines as a short list with their year set as a small mint-free green number (`#0A5150`, 9.1:1), each linking to its Instagram post. ~1,000 px.
7. **The method: "four photo panels", full-bleed on `#0A5150`** (L4). A row of four tall photo panels edge to edge: SENSING img-003, THINKING img-032, MAKING vid-004 (poster + clip), INFLUENCING img-011. Each panel has its Latin label and Hebrew `title` in white on the photo's lower third, on a deep-green gradient that belongs to the panel, not over a hero. On desktop, hovering or focusing a panel widens it from 25 % to 40 % (300 ms) and reveals its `body`. ### Concept 2 · interactive — **"בחרו פורמט" (Pick a format)**

**The idea in one sentence.** The first screen is the booking step. **The one thing.** **Her card deck** (img-008, img-024, img-032: white cards with one word, "change", held over the box).
