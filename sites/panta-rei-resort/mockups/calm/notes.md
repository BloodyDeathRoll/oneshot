## gaps

- **No room prices** (engine needs dates): no "from" price anywhere; every room has a one-tap link to its own engine page, and the drawer passes dates and guests. The engine's `start` / `end` parameters were verified on 2026-11-06 (Playwright on `/listings/572800?start=2026-12-10&end=2026-12-13&numberOfGuests=2` showed "Check-in Dec 10 · Check-out Dec 13 · $465 for 3 nights"); `numberOfGuests` is passed but its effect was not separately verified.
- **No Instagram images** in the dossier: custom.md's Instagram feed is replaced by the Instagram icon button in the footer.
- **No reply time, no staff names, no named break, no season**: none shown; no forecast block (concept §3).
- **Reviews carry no dates** in copy (`testimonials[].date: null`): shown with name and source only.
- **Testimonial "Alba R"** is sourced as `site` (the business's own site): labelled "on our website".
- Tagline (what/where) is not on the first screen: the hero carries only "Panta Rei, Kabalana" + one button (custom.md); the tagline opens the Welcome section.

## conflicts

- **Closing still** (minor, not blocking): concept §4.1 says "vid-002 poster full-bleed". That poster is a top-down of the ring nearly identical to the hero poster, so the closing would repeat the first screen. I used a later frame of the same vid-002 (16 s: the ring among the palms with the sea beyond), lifted with gamma 1.18 in the recipe. Alternative cost: a closing that reads as the hero again.
- **Hero on portrait phones** (minor): concept names the vid-001 cut; on phones I serve a 9:16 crop of the same cut from the 1080 p source (`vid-001-p608.mp4`, 1.94 MB) instead of the 1280×720 rendition, which was 2.3× upscaled into a portrait screen and looked soft (round 2). Same footage, same 55–70 s.
- **"Guests" heading**: the copy has no heading for the reviews block; "What guests say" (and "Good to know" in Getting here) are interface labels, not business claims.
- **Concept item 6 "price rows compact"** built as tinted rows rather than " | " joined text, because three class prices and four massages each carry a note/unit; rooms' details do use " | ".
- Hard constraint 5 (other businesses): this run has one business, so there is no other business's mockup to differ from. The sibling variants (interactive: Fredoka + Nunito Sans; unique: Bricolage Grotesque + Hanken Grotesk, per check-design) were not opened as design and were not touched.
- Inspector output was written by the skill to `.taste-skill/inspect/` at the repo root (gitignored scratch; the skill's fixed location), outside this folder.
- No blocking conflict. Visual-critic scores are not in yet (4f); nothing here predicts them.
