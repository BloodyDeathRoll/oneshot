**Subject:** Sanjee Surf School: a one-page website preview, and what it would give up

Hello,

We don't know who looks after sanjee-surf.com or who decides for the school, so this is written to the school. If that is someone else, please pass it on.

sanjee-surf.com is a real, working site, with 67 pages and a dialog that sells a lesson with live dates, named times and a deposit. We walked it on a phone on 5 November, as a traveller would, and built a one-page alternative from the school's published facts and photographs. It is a private preview, hidden from search engines, and sanjee-surf.com stays as it is:
https://sanjee-surf-school.os1.workers.dev

What a visitor meets on a phone today:
- Booking takes five taps, three typed fields and a card deposit, and the dialog has no WhatsApp or phone link.
- The home page has no phone or WhatsApp link. Its contact block gives the location as "Tropical Beach, Paradise Coast" and prints an email on a domain with no mail record (checked 5 November).
- Lesson prices and cancellation terms differ between pages, and the Instagram, Facebook and YouTube links lead nowhere for a logged-out visitor.
- A red "Could not refresh surf conditions" box covered the top of the first screen on all 3 fresh loads we made, from one machine and one network. The page's code suggests other visitors get it too, but we have not tested that. The proposal says what we found.

What the preview gives up: the current dialog holds a time and takes an $8 deposit. The preview ends in a question on WhatsApp. It doesn't offer a time of day, holds nothing, takes no money, and leaves out the blog, the FAQ pages and the surf report. We have not messaged +94 77 400 4422, so we don't know whether it is answered or how fast. We don't claim it will bring more bookings, and it has not been tested on a real iPhone or a range of Android phones. That trade is yours to weigh.

The details, with screenshots, are on the proposal page, along with a list of things only the school can confirm, such as hours, cancellation and two photographs:
https://sanjee-surf-school-proposal.os1.workers.dev

It could run in three ways, your choice. The one we recommend: we host the site and keep it running (hosting, updates and backups are on us), and you pay a monthly fee and edit the content yourselves. The second: we install the same site and the same editor on a server of your own for a one-time fee. It is entirely yours; you pay the server company roughly $6–12 a month, and updates and backups are on you. The third, if you never want to use an editor: we hand you the finished site as files that run on any web host, and every change then needs a developer. We'd agree the prices together. Nothing is set up yet, and there is no login or editor today.

Nothing goes live unless you decide. Want to hear more, or would you rather we took the preview down? Just reply to this email.
