# In person — The Lavish Haven (English)

For showing the preview on a phone, face to face at the front desk. Open https://the-lavish-haven.os1.workers.dev/ before you walk in, and make sure you have mobile data: the forecast and photos load as you scroll. Run is `mode: test`: nothing is used until a human decides to go. Sources: `notes.md`.

Guests name the owner as Amal in Booking.com reviews (unconfirmed). Ask for the owner or manager; don't assume the name.

## Opening line

"Hello. The Lavish Haven doesn't have a website yet, so we built one from your Booking.com and Google listings. Can I show you on my phone? It takes two minutes."

## Points (scroll as you go)

1. **First screen.** "Your name, 'a new hotel, two minutes from Kabalana', and both ratings: 9.3 on Booking.com, 5.0 on Google, with the date we read them. The yellow 'Check dates' button stays on screen and goes straight to your Booking.com page." (Booking.com showed 9.3 on 25 September; say we will update it.)
2. **Rooms and photos.** Scroll to "Nine kinds of room" and the photo strip. "All nine room types from your listing, your photos, and your guests' own words, copied exactly."
3. **Fast on a phone.** "On a slow mobile connection the first screen shows in under half a second in our test." No other number needed.
4. **What they could add.** "We only used what's public. We couldn't find your check-in times, a price list, an email or a WhatsApp number. If you have a WhatsApp number, we can add a 'message us' button for you."
5. **They edit it.** "You get your own login. Rooms, photos, text, hours, prices, contact: you change them yourself, and in our test a change was live about 17 seconds later. The design can't break."

## The offer (say it plainly)

"Two ways. Managed: we host it and keep it running, updates and backups are ours, for a monthly fee we agree with you. Or self-hosted: we set it up on your own server for a one-time fee we agree with you, it's fully yours, you pay your server company roughly 6 to 12 dollars a month, and you look after updates and backups."

Recommend managed. Don't mention a static-only version unless they say they never want to edit.

## If they ask "How much?"

"That's something we agree with you, not a fixed list price. Managed is a monthly fee; self-hosted is a one-time fee plus your own server bill. First tell us whether you like the site and what you'd change, and then we agree the figure together."

Do not give a number. No fee amounts exist yet.

## The ask

"Would you like to keep it? You can look at it again on your own: I'll send you the link on WhatsApp. Reply there, or tell us when we come by."

- Does +94 76 483 2988 have WhatsApp? Should that be the booking number?
- Once the site is public, should AI assistants be allowed to read it? (default: yes)

## Don't

- Don't promise more bookings, a domain, or a place on Google. The preview is hidden from search for now.
- Don't criticise the listings or name other hotels.
- Don't press. The preview stays private until they decide.
