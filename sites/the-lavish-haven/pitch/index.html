Before and after

Today

A guest looking for you finds booking listings and a map pin: Booking.com, Agoda, Tripadvisor and Google Maps. There is no website and no social media account where the hotel speaks for itself.

Guests like what they find there: 9.3 out of 10 from 34 reviews on Booking.com and 5.0 out of 5 from 21 reviews on Google (read 25 September 2026). What we could not find published anywhere: a price list, a WhatsApp number and an email address.

With the new site

One page about The Lavish Haven only: your twelve room types, your photos, both ratings, guests' own words, the beach and the wave, and a "Check dates" button on every screen that goes to your Booking.com page.

What you could add that guests can't find today

We only used what is already public. You know more than your listings say, and each of these has its own place in the site, ready to fill in:

  • A WhatsApp number. You can save it in your login. Showing it on the page as a "message us" button is a small design change we make for you.
  • Prices. There is no published price list. You can add one, or keep letting the booking sites show rates by date.
  • An email address and your own words about the hotel. We found no description written by you online; the one on Booking.com is Booking.com's own summary.

What you can change yourself

You get your own login to a simple admin page. It only shows The Lavish Haven. You fill in fields, not code, so an edit can't break the design. When we tested it, a change saved in the admin was live on the site 17 seconds later.

  • Basics name, headline lines, languages you speak
  • Text intro, about, what you offer, good to know
  • Opening hours hours and a short note
  • Prices a price list, if you want one
  • Photos upload, remove and reorder
  • Rooms & stay room types, beds, check-in and check-out times
  • Amenities the facilities list and notes like "additional charge"
  • Surf your words about the wave and the season
  • Reviews ratings and guest quotes are copied word for word from the platforms. You can remove any of them; new ones are added by us, so they always match the source.
  • Contact & booking phone, WhatsApp, email, address, map link, booking links
  • Lessons and Rentals & shop are there too, left empty for a hotel.

Design, layout and the technical setup stay fixed. That part is ours to look after.

A few things to check with you

  1. Your house number. Google lists No166/1 Walhengoda Road, Booking.com lists 166/2 Welhengoda Road. The site follows Google for now. Which one is right?
  2. Your name. Google calls you "The Lavish Haven Hotel And Resort", Booking.com "The Lavish Haven". The site uses the short one. Is that how you want it?
  3. AI assistants. Once the site is public, it is set to let AI assistants read and quote it, for example when a guest asks where to stay near Kabalana. You can keep that, or block it.

The offer

There are two ways to keep the site, and the choice is yours. Managed is the one we recommend: we host the site and keep it running, and hosting, updates and backups are our job. You pay a monthly fee that we agree with you, and you edit your content whenever you like. Self-hosted: we install the same site and editor on a server of your own for a one-time fee that we agree with you. It is then fully yours. You pay your server company roughly $6–12 a month, and you take care of updates and backups yourself.

The site is on a preview address for now. If you already own a web address, we connect it.
