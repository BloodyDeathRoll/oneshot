## Warm water, mellow waves

Imaginary Bay is a sandy-bottom beach break that stays waist high most mornings, which makes it a forgiving place to catch a first wave.

Our coaches have taught hundreds of nervous beginners. Boards, rash vests and a post-surf coconut are included in every lesson.
